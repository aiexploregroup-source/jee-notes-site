What is JEE Notes?

JEE Notes is a free study platform for students preparing for the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE Main and Advanced). It covers the full syllabus — 20 Physics chapters, 20 Chemistry chapters, and 14 Mathematics chapters — with concept notes, formula sheets, solved previous-year questions, full-length mock tests, and a daily challenge.

Everything on this site is free, with no signup, no login, and no paywall. You can read, print, or copy any page as Markdown.

Who runs it

JEE Notes is operated by HappyChases Media Works (OPC) Private Limited, an Indian company. We are an independent study resource — not affiliated with the NTA, any IIT, or any coaching institute (see the full disclaimer).

How the content is made — and checked

We believe you should know exactly how the material you study was produced:

  • Concept notes are written against the current NTA syllabus, structured for exam preparation: core concepts from first principles, worked formulas with their conditions of validity, memory aids, common-mistake warnings, and practice problems at three difficulty levels.
  • Previous-year questions and mock tests use real JEE Main papers. Every MCQ answer is validated against the official NTA final answer key.
  • Worked solutions are drafted with the help of AI tools and are being progressively reviewed for step-by-step correctness. Pages whose solutions are still in the review queue say so. We would rather tell you this plainly than pretend otherwise — for a definitive answer, the NTA key is always the authority, and we mark our solutions as ours.

Found an error?

Mistakes in exam-prep material cost marks, so we treat error reports as the highest-priority input. If a solution step, formula, or answer looks wrong:

  • Leave a comment on the page (every topic page has a comment section), or
  • Reach us through the contact details in our terms — error reports are read and actioned ahead of everything else.

Corrections ship in the next daily site build.

Why trust a free site?

Fair question. Our answer: check us. Every solution shows its working so you can verify each step against your own; every answer can be checked against the official NTA key; and our terms, privacy policy, and disclaimer name the company and grievance contact behind the site. We keep the site free because the material costs us little to host — and a student in a small town with no coaching budget deserves the same quality of revision material as anyone else.
