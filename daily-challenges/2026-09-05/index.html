Daily Challenge Saturday, 5 September 2026

Daily Challenge — 5 September 2026

Week 5 of Mission JEE 2027 · Spin City — Rotation is JEE's favourite mechanics chapter; thermo is chemistry's; trigonometry is everywhere. See the full plan.

Watch a leg-spinner’s wrist at the point of release - the ball leaves with rotation baked in, and everything after that, the drift, the dip, the vicious turn off the pitch, is torque and angular momentum arguing with each other in real time. Rotational motion, six deliveries an over.

Between overs the chemistry happens at the boundary rope tea stall, where the first law of thermodynamics still refuses to let a single joule from that stove go unaccounted. And the angle a full toss takes off the bat is a trigonometric equation with a finite number of solutions - much like your patience for a slow over.

Read the wrist, then read the question. Three of them today, all catchable - go.

Physics

Rotational Motion

JEE Main 2025 Morning

A force of 49 N acts tangentially at the highest point of a sphere (solid) of mass 20 kg , kept on a rough horizontal plane. If the sphere rolls without slipping, then the acceleration of the center of the sphere is

Study this chapter →

Torque about bottom point

$$\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{F} \times 2 \mathrm{r}=\mathrm{I} \alpha \\ & 49 \times 2 \mathrm{r}=\frac{7}{5} \mathrm{mr}^2 \alpha \\ & 14=4 \mathrm{r} \alpha \end{aligned}$$

As sphere rolls without slipping

$$\begin{aligned} & \mathrm{a}=\mathrm{r} \alpha \\ & \mathrm{a}=\frac{14}{4}=\frac{7}{2}=3.5 \mathrm{~m} / \mathrm{s}^2 \end{aligned}$$

Chemical Thermodynamics

JEE Main 2026 8 Apr, Shift 2

Consider the reaction

$$2H_2S(g) + 3O_2(g) \rightarrow 2H_2O(l) + 2SO_2(g)$$

The magnitude of enthalpy change for the reaction in kJ mol$^{-1}$ is __________. (Nearest integer)

Given: $\Delta_f H^{\ominus}(H_2S) = -20.1$ kJ mol$^{-1}$, $\Delta_f H^{\ominus}(H_2O) = -286.0$ kJ mol$^{-1}$, $\Delta_f H^{\ominus}(SO_2) = -297.0$ kJ mol$^{-1}$.

Numerical answer — work it out, then check the solution.

Study this chapter →

Using $\Delta_r H = \sum \Delta_f H_{\text{products}} - \sum \Delta_f H_{\text{reactants}}$, with $\Delta_f H(\mathrm{O_2}) = 0$:

$$\Delta_r H = \big[2(-286.0) + 2(-297.0)\big] - \big[2(-20.1) + 3(0)\big]$$$$= (-572.0 - 594.0) - (-40.2) = -1166.0 + 40.2 = -1125.8 \text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$$

Magnitude $= 1125.8 \approx 1126$.

Answer: 1126

Trigonometric Functions and Equations

JEE Main 2025 Morning
If $\theta \in[-2 \pi, 2 \pi]$, then the number of solutions of $2 \sqrt{2} \cos ^2 \theta+(2-\sqrt{6}) \cos \theta-\sqrt{3}=0$, is equal to:
Study this chapter →
$$\begin{aligned} & 2 \sqrt{2} \cos ^2 \theta+(2-\sqrt{6}) \cos \theta-\sqrt{3}=0 \\ & 2 \sqrt{2} \cos ^2 \theta+2 \cos \theta-\sqrt{6} \cos \theta-\sqrt{3}=0 \\ & (2 \cos \theta-\sqrt{3})(\sqrt{2} \cos \theta+1)=0 \\ & \Rightarrow \cos \theta=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \text { or } \cos \theta=\frac{-1}{\sqrt{2}} \\ & \theta=\left\{\frac{-11 \pi}{6}, \frac{-5 \pi}{4}, \frac{-3 \pi}{4}, \frac{-\pi}{6}, \frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{3 \pi}{4}, \frac{5 \pi}{4}, \frac{11 \pi}{6}\right\} \\ & \Rightarrow 8 \text { (solution) } \end{aligned}$$
