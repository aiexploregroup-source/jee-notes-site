Daily Challenge — 2 September 2026

Week 5 of Mission JEE 2027 · Spin City — Rotation is JEE's favourite mechanics chapter; thermo is chemistry's; trigonometry is everywhere. See the full plan.

Chhava spends its battle scenes on formations that wheel and pivot around a fixed point, cavalry sweeping wide while the center holds - that is a rigid body rotating about an axis, dressed up in seventeenth century armour instead of a JEE diagram.

Every siege in that film also runs on a thermodynamics budget nobody states out loud: the energy in the grain stores has to last the energy the army spends, first law, no exceptions, kings included. And the angle at which a spear meets a shield is trigonometry deciding whether it glances off or lands.

History does not skip the syllabus. Take on today’s three questions like the formation depends on it.

Chemistry

Chemical Thermodynamics

JEE Main 2026 5 Apr, Shift 2

Consider the following data for the reaction $X_2(g) + Y_2(g) \rightleftharpoons 2XY(g)$ at 600 K. The $\Delta_r G^\circ$ (in kJ mol$^{-1}$) for the reaction is:

Compound$\Delta_f H^\circ_{600K}$ (kJ mol$^{-1}$)$S^\circ_{600K}$ (J mol$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$)
XY(g)42200
X$_2$(g)8140
Y$_2$(g)80250
Study this chapter →

Enthalpy of reaction:

$$\Delta_r H^\circ = 2(42) - (8 + 80) = 84 - 88 = -4 \text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$$

Entropy of reaction:

$$\Delta_r S^\circ = 2(200) - (140 + 250) = 400 - 390 = 10 \text{ J mol}^{-1}\text{K}^{-1}$$

Gibbs free energy at 600 K:

$$\Delta_r G^\circ = \Delta_r H^\circ - T\Delta_r S^\circ = -4 - 600\times\frac{10}{1000} = -4 - 6 = -10 \text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$$

Answer: $-10$ (B)

Trigonometric Ratio and Identities
