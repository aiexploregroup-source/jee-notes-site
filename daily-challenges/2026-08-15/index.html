Physics

Kinematics

JEE Main 2026 8 Apr, Shift 2
A gas balloon is going up with a constant velocity of 10 m/s. When this balloon reached a height of 75 m, a stone is dropped from it and balloon keeps moving up with the same velocity. The height of the balloon when the stone hits the ground is __________ m. (Take $g = 10$ m/s$^2$)
Study this chapter →

When released, the stone shares the balloon’s upward velocity, so its initial velocity is $+10$ m/s (upward). Take upward as positive with the origin at the ground.

Stone’s position: $y = 75 + 10t - 5t^2$. It hits the ground when $y = 0$:

$$5t^2 - 10t - 75 = 0 \implies t^2 - 2t - 15 = 0$$$$t = \frac{2 + \sqrt{4 + 60}}{2} = \frac{2 + 8}{2} = 5\ \text{s}$$

Meanwhile the balloon rises at $10$ m/s from $75$ m:

$$h_{\text{balloon}} = 75 + 10 \times 5 = 125\ \text{m}$$

Answer: D (125)

Structure of Atom

JEE Main 2025 Evening

Which of the following statements are true?

(A) The subsidiary quantum number $l$ describes the shape of the orbital occupied by the electron.

(B) is the boundary surface diagram of the $2 \mathrm{p}_x$ orbital.

(C) The + and - signs in the wave function of the $2 p_x$ orbital refer to charge.

(D) The wave function of $2 \mathrm{p}_x$ orbital is zero everywhere in the $x y$ plane.

Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

Study this chapter →

(A) Azimuthal quantum number ( $\ell$ ) indicates the shape of orbital occupied by the electron

(C) The + and - sign in the wave function of $2 p_x$ orbital refer to the sign (Phase) of the wave function, not the charge

(D) The wave function of $2 p_x$ orbital will be zero in yz plane (Nodal plane).

Quadratic Equation and Inequalities

JEE Main 2025 Evening
If the set of all $a \in \mathbf{R}$, for which the equation $2 x^2+(a-5) x+15=3 a$ has no real root, is the interval ( $\alpha, \beta$ ), and $X=|x \in Z ; \alpha < x < \beta|$, then $\sum\limits_{x \in X} x^2$ is equal to:
Study this chapter →
$$\begin{aligned} & (a-5)^2-8(15-3 a)<0 \\ & a^2+14 a+25-120<0 \\ & a^2+14 a-95<0 \\ & (a+19)(a-5)<0 \\ & a \in(-19,5) \\ & \therefore-19< x<5 \\ & \therefore \sum_{x \in X} x^2=\left(1^2+2^2+\ldots .+4^2\right)+\left(1^2+2^2+\ldots+18^2\right) \\ & =\frac{4 \times 5 \times 9}{6}+\frac{18 \times 19 \times 37}{6} \\ & =30+2109 \\ & =2139 \end{aligned}$$
