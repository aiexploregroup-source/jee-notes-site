Physics

Thermodynamics

JEE Main 2026 Apr 2, Shift 1
Heat is supplied to a diatomic gas at constant pressure. Then the ratio of $\Delta Q : \Delta U : \Delta W$ is __________.
Study this chapter →

For a diatomic gas, $C_V = \dfrac{5}{2}R$ and $C_P = \dfrac{7}{2}R$. At constant pressure, for $n$ moles over $\Delta T$:

$$\Delta Q = nC_P\,\Delta T = \frac{7}{2}nR\,\Delta T$$

$$\Delta U = nC_V\,\Delta T = \frac{5}{2}nR\,\Delta T$$

$$\Delta W = P\,\Delta V = nR\,\Delta T$$

Dividing each by $nR\,\Delta T$:

$$\Delta Q : \Delta U : \Delta W = \frac{7}{2} : \frac{5}{2} : 1 = 7 : 5 : 2$$

Answer: D ($7 : 5 : 2$)

Chemistry

Solutions

JEE Main 2025 Evening
$\mathrm{HA}(a q) \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{H}^{+}(a q)+\mathrm{A}^{-}(a q)$

The freezing point depression of a 0.1 m aqueous solution of a monobasic weak acid HA is 0.20 °C. The dissociation constant for the acid is

Given: $K_f$(H2O) = 1.8 K kg mol−1, molality ≡ molarity

Study this chapter →

Freezing Point Depression:

$ \Delta T_f = i \times K_f \times m $

Given:

$ \Delta T_f = 0.2 \, \text{°C}, \quad K_f = 1.8 \, \text{K kg mol}^{-1}, \quad m = 0.1 \, \text{m} $

Substituting the given values:

$ 0.2 = i \times 1.8 \times 0.1 $

Solving for $i$:

$ i = \frac{0.2}{1.8 \times 0.1} = \frac{20}{18} = \frac{10}{9} $

Degree of Dissociation ($\alpha$):

For the reaction $\mathrm{HA} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{H}^{+} + \mathrm{A}^{-}$:

$ i = 1 + \alpha $

Given $i = \frac{10}{9}$:

$ \frac{10}{9} = 1 + \alpha $

$ \alpha = \frac{1}{9} $

Dissociation Constant ($K_{eq}$):

$ \mathrm{K}_{eq} = \frac{[H^+][A^-]}{[HA]} $

At equilibrium:

$ [H^+] = [A^-] = \alpha \times C = \frac{1}{9} \times 0.1 $

$ [HA] = 0.1 \times (1 - \alpha) = 0.1 \times \left(1 - \frac{1}{9}\right) $

Substituting these into $K_{eq}$:

$ \mathrm{K}_{eq} = \frac{(0.1 \times \frac{1}{9})^2}{0.1 \times \left(1 - \frac{1}{9}\right)} $

Simplifying:

$ \mathrm{K}_{eq} = \frac{0.1 \times \left(\frac{1}{81}\right)}{0.1 \times \frac{8}{9}} = \frac{1}{720} $

Therefore:

$ \mathrm{K}_{eq} = 1.38 \times 10^{-3} $

Sequences and Series

JEE Main 2026 5 Apr, Shift 2
Let $A_1, A_2, A_3, \dots, A_{39}$ be 39 arithmetic means between the numbers 59 and 159. Then the mean of $A_{25}, A_{28}, A_{31}$ and $A_{36}$ is equal to :
Study this chapter →

Inserting $39$ arithmetic means between $59$ and $159$ gives an A.P. of $41$ terms:

$$59, A_1, A_2, \dots, A_{39}, 159.$$

Common difference:

$$d = \frac{159 - 59}{40} = \frac{100}{40} = \frac{5}{2}.$$

Then $A_k = 59 + k\cdot\dfrac{5}{2}$. The required mean is

$$\frac{A_{25} + A_{28} + A_{31} + A_{36}}{4} = 59 + \frac{5}{2}\cdot\frac{25 + 28 + 31 + 36}{4} = 59 + \frac{5}{2}\cdot\frac{120}{4}.$$$$= 59 + \frac{5}{2}\cdot 30 = 59 + 75 = 134.$$

Answer: D (134)
