# Mission JEE 2027 — The Complete Study Plan

A free week-by-week JEE 2027 study plan from August 2026 to JEE Advanced: full syllabus in 24 weeks, revision cycles, exam-window tapers, and a daily challenge aligned to every week.

This is the plan the [Daily Challenge](../daily-challenges/index.md) runs on: **42 weeks from August 2026 to JEE Advanced 2027**, with every day’s three questions drawn from the week you’re supposed to be studying. Follow the plan and the challenge quietly becomes your revision engine — cover a chapter, then get tested on it while it’s fresh, then meet it again in review weeks and revision cycles exactly when you’re about to forget it.

## How the plan works

- **Phase 1 — Full syllabus (Weeks 1–24, Aug to mid-Jan):** one chapter theme per subject per week, ordered by dependency and exam weightage. Review weeks consolidate each block before you move on.
- **Exam windows (Weeks 25–26 and 35–36):** during JEE Main sessions the challenge switches to light, familiar reps — stay sharp without burning out.
- **Phase 2 — Revision cycles (Weeks 27–34, Feb–Mar):** weightage-ordered clusters, a weak-spot week that resurfaces what you’ve seen least recently, and two PYQ marathons.
- **Phase 3 — Advanced mode (Weeks 37–42, Apr–May):** numerical-heavy, multi-concept days tuned for JEE Advanced, ending in a deliberate taper.

Study each week’s chapters from the [notes](../physics/index.md), run the chapter’s [formula sheet](../formula-sheets/index.md) and [previous year questions](../previous-year-questions/index.md), do the daily challenge every day, and take a [mock test](../mock-tests/index.md) every week or two from October onward. That’s the whole system.
