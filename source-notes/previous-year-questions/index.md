# JEE Main Previous Year Questions — Chapter Wise

Chapter-wise JEE Main previous year questions with step-by-step worked solutions, for every Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics chapter. Free, no signup.

Practicing real exam questions chapter-by-chapter is the fastest way to find out whether a chapter is actually exam-ready. Every chapter below links to a dedicated page of **JEE Main previous year questions with complete worked solutions** — answers checked against the official NTA answer keys, solutions written step-by-step so you can find exactly where your attempt diverged.

Use them at the end of each chapter’s revision: attempt every question under time pressure first, then read the solutions for the ones you missed. For full-paper practice under exam conditions, head to the [mock tests](../mock-tests/index.md) instead, and for a daily three-question warm-up try the [Daily Challenge](../daily-challenges/index.md).
