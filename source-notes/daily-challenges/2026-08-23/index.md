Physics

## Laws of Motion

JEE Main 2026
8 Apr, Shift 2

A car moving with a speed of 54 km/h takes a turn of radius 20 m. A simple pendulum is suspended from the ceiling of the car. Determine the angle made by the string of the pendulum with the vertical during the turning. (Take $g = 10$ m/s$^2$)

- **A.** $\tan^{-1}(0.5)$

- **B.** $\tan^{-1}(0.75)$

- **C.** $\tan^{-1}(1.125)$

- **D.** $\tan^{-1}(0.25)$

**Solution / Answer**

Convert the speed: $v = 54\ \text{km/h} = 54 \times \dfrac{5}{18} = 15\ \text{m/s}$.

In the car’s (non-inertial) frame, the bob experiences a pseudo (centrifugal) force $\dfrac{mv^2}{r}$ horizontally outward and gravity $mg$ downward. At equilibrium the string tilts by angle $\theta$ from the vertical:

$$\tan\theta = \frac{mv^2/r}{mg} = \frac{v^2}{rg}$$

**Substitute values:**

$$\tan\theta = \frac{(15)^2}{20 \times 10} = \frac{225}{200} = 1.125$$

$$\theta = \tan^{-1}(1.125)$$

**Answer:** C ($\tan^{-1}(1.125)$)

**Correct option:** C

## Classification of Elements and Periodicity

JEE Main 2026
4 Apr, Shift 1

A monoatomic anion ($A^-$) has 45 neutrons and 36 electrons. Atomic mass, group in the periodic table and physical state at room temperature of the element (A) respectively are

- **A.** 80, 17, liquid

- **B.** 81, 16, solid

- **C.** 80, 16, gas

- **D.** 81, 15, gas

**Solution / Answer**

The anion $A^-$ carries one extra electron, so the neutral atom has

$$Z = \text{electrons in } A^- - 1 = 36 - 1 = 35$$

An atomic number $Z = 35$ corresponds to **bromine (Br)**.

$$\text{Atomic mass} = Z + \text{neutrons} = 35 + 45 = 80$$

Bromine’s configuration is $[\text{Ar}]\,3d^{10}\,4s^2\,4p^5$, placing it in **Group 17** (halogens). Elemental bromine $\left(\text{Br}_2\right)$ is a reddish-brown **liquid** at room temperature.

**Answer:** A (80, 17, liquid)

**Correct option:** A

## Sequences and Series

JEE Main 2025
Evening

If $7=5+\frac{1}{7}(5+\alpha)+\frac{1}{7^2}(5+2 \alpha)+\frac{1}{7^3}(5+3 \alpha)+\ldots \ldots \ldots \ldots \infty$, then the value of $\alpha$ is :

- **A.** $\frac{1}{7}$

- **B.** 1

- **C.** $\frac{6}{7}$

- **D.** 6

**Solution / Answer**

$$\begin{aligned} & \text { Let } S=5+\frac{1}{7}(5+\alpha)+\frac{1}{7^2}(5+2 \alpha)+\ldots \\ & \frac{1}{7} S=\frac{1}{7}(5)+\frac{1}{7^2}(5+\alpha)+\ldots \infty \end{aligned}$$

$$\begin{aligned} &\frac{6}{7}(S)=5+\frac{1}{7} \alpha\left(\frac{1}{1-\frac{1}{7}}\right)\\ &6=5+\frac{\alpha}{6} \Rightarrow \alpha=6 \end{aligned}$$

**Correct option:** D
