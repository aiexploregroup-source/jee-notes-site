Look up. That ceiling fan regulator you nudge from 3 to 5 every summer evening is you personally adjusting angular velocity, and the three seconds it takes to spin up to full speed is angular acceleration doing its job. Rotational motion is not a chapter, it is your bedroom.

Downstairs the kettle is running the first law of thermodynamics on your family’s chai budget, and the shadow the fan blade throws on the wall traces out a sine curve every single rotation, which is trigonometry moonlighting as home decor.

Physics

## Rotational Motion

JEE Main 2026
2 Apr, Shift 1

A particle is rotating in a circular path and at any instant its motion can be described as $\theta = \dfrac{5t^4}{40} - \dfrac{t^3}{3}$. The angular acceleration of the particle after 10 seconds is __________ rad/s$^2$.

- **A.** 150

- **B.** 120

- **C.** 130

- **D.** 170

**Solution / Answer**

Simplify $\theta = \dfrac{t^4}{8} - \dfrac{t^3}{3}$.

**Angular velocity:**

$$\omega = \frac{d\theta}{dt} = \frac{t^3}{2} - t^2$$

**Angular acceleration:**

$$\alpha = \frac{d\omega}{dt} = \frac{3t^2}{2} - 2t$$

**At $t = 10$ s:**

$$\alpha = \frac{3(100)}{2} - 2(10) = 150 - 20 = 130\ \text{rad/s}^2$$

**Answer:** C (130)

**Correct option:** C

## Thermodynamics

JEE Main 2025
Evening

The hydration energies of $K^+$ and $Cl^-$ are $-x$ and $-y$ kJ/mol respectively. If the lattice energy of KCl is $-z$ kJ/mol, then the heat of solution of KCl is :

- **A.** $x + y + z$

- **B.** $z - (x + y)$

- **C.** $-z - (x + y)$

- **D.** $x - y - z$

**Solution / Answer**

$\mathrm{KCl}_{(\mathrm{s})}+\mathrm{H}_2 \mathrm{O} \xrightarrow{\Delta \mathrm{H} \text { sol. }} \mathrm{K}_{(\mathrm{aq})}^{+}+\mathrm{Cl}_{\text {(aq) }}^{-1}$

![](https://app-content.cdn.examgoal.net/fly/@width/image/jaoe38c1m9jkrctw/c67cc0c7-f522-4a63-934a-5dfdb5172eda/6db04740-1a8f-11f0-99a3-8966777a8fdf/file-jaoe38c1m9jkrctx.png)

$$\begin{aligned} \Delta \mathrm{H}_{\mathrm{Sol}^{\mathrm{n}} .} & =\mathrm{L} \cdot \mathrm{E} \cdot+(\mathrm{H} \cdot \mathrm{E})_{\mathrm{K}_{(\mathrm{g})}^{+}}+(\mathrm{HE})_{\mathrm{Cl}_{(\mathrm{g})}^{-1}} \\ & =\mathrm{Z}-\mathrm{x}-\mathrm{y} \\ & =\mathrm{z}-(\mathrm{x}+\mathrm{y}) \end{aligned}$$

**Correct option:** B

## Trigonometric Ratio and Identities

JEE Main 2025
Evening

If $\sin x + \sin^2 x = 1$, $x \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$, then

$(\cos^{12} x + \tan^{12} x) + 3(\cos^{10} x + \tan^{10} x + \cos^8 x + \tan^8 x) + (\cos^6 x + \tan^6 x)$ is equal to:

- **A.** 3

- **B.** 4

- **C.** 2

- **D.** 1

**Solution / Answer**

$$\begin{aligned} &\begin{aligned} & \sin x+\sin ^2 x=1 \\ & \Rightarrow \sin x=\cos ^2 x \Rightarrow \tan x=\cos x \end{aligned}\\ &\therefore \text { Given expression }\\ &\begin{aligned} & =2 \cos ^{12} x+6\left[\cos ^{10} x+\cos ^8 x\right]+2 \cos ^6 x \\ & =2\left[\sin ^6 x+3 \sin ^5 x+3 \sin ^4 x+\sin ^3 x\right] \\ & =2 \sin ^3 x\left[(\sin x+1)^3\right] \\ & =2\left[\sin ^2 x+\sin x\right]^3 \\ & =2 \end{aligned} \end{aligned}$$

**Correct option:** C
