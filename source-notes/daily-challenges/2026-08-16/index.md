## Kinematics

JEE Main 2026
8 Apr, Shift 2

Two identical bodies, projected with the same speed at two different angles cover the same horizontal range $R$. If the time of flight of these bodies are 5 s and 10 s, respectively, then the value of $R$ is __________ m. (Take $g = 10$ m/s$^2$)

- **A.** 250

- **B.** 25

- **C.** 500

- **D.** 125

**Solution / Answer**

Equal ranges at the same speed occur for complementary launch angles $\theta$ and $90^\circ - \theta$. Their times of flight are

$$T_1 = \frac{2u\sin\theta}{g}, \qquad T_2 = \frac{2u\cos\theta}{g}$$

Multiplying:

$$T_1 T_2 = \frac{4u^2\sin\theta\cos\theta}{g^2} = \frac{2u^2\sin 2\theta}{g^2} = \frac{2R}{g}$$

since $R = \dfrac{u^2\sin 2\theta}{g}$. Solving for $R$:

$$R = \frac{g\,T_1 T_2}{2} = \frac{10 \times 5 \times 10}{2} = 250\ \text{m}$$

**Answer:** A (250)

**Correct option:** A

## Structure of Atom

Radius of the first excited state of Helium ion is given as : $\mathrm{a}_0 \rightarrow$ radius of first stationary state of hydrogen atom.

- **A.** $\mathrm{r}=\frac{\mathrm{a}_0}{4}$

- **B.** $\mathrm{r}=2 \mathrm{a}_0$

- **C.** $\mathrm{r}=4 \mathrm{a}_0$

- **D.** $\mathrm{r=\frac{a_0}{2}}$

**Solution / Answer**

$$ r_n = \frac{n^2\, a_0}{Z} $$

For the helium ion, which is hydrogen-like with a nuclear charge of $$ Z = 2 $$, the first excited state corresponds to $$ n = 2 $$. Substituting these values:

$$ r_2 = \frac{(2)^2\, a_0}{2} = \frac{4\, a_0}{2} = 2\, a_0 $$

Thus, the radius of the first excited state of the helium ion is $$ 2\, a_0 $$.

**Correct option:** B

## Quadratic Equation and Inequalities

JEE Main 2025
Morning

Let the set of all values of $p \in \mathbb{R}$, for which both the roots of the equation $x^2-(p+2) x+(2 p+9)=0$ are negative real numbers, be the interval $(\alpha, \beta]$. Then $\beta-2 \alpha$ is equal to

- **A.** 5

- **B.** 0

- **C.** 20

- **D.** 9

**Solution / Answer**

To find the set of all values of $ p \in \mathbb{R} $ for which both roots of the equation $ x^2-(p+2)x+(2p+9)=0 $ are negative real numbers, follow these steps:

**Discriminant Condition**:

The equation's discriminant $ D $ must be non-negative for real roots:

$ (p+2)^2 - 4(2p+9) \geq 0 $

Simplifying this:

$ p^2 + 4p + 4 - 8p - 36 \geq 0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad p^2 - 4p - 32 \geq 0 $

This can be factored as:

$ (p-8)(p+4) \geq 0 $

Meaning $ p \in (-\infty, -4] \cup [8, \infty) $. …… (1)

**Sum of Roots Condition**:

The sum of the roots (which is $ p+2 $) must be negative:

$ p + 2 < 0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad p < -2 $ …… (2)

**Product of Roots Condition**:

The product of the roots $ (2p + 9) $ must be positive:

$ 2p + 9 > 0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad p > -\frac{9}{2} $ …… (3)

**Determine the Valid Interval**:

Combine the results from conditions (1), (2), and (3). From conditions (1) and (2), we find $ p < -2 $:

Intersection of $ (-\infty, -4] $ and $ (-\frac{9}{2}, -2) $ gives:

$ p \in \left(-\frac{9}{2}, -4\right] $

**Calculate $\beta - 2\alpha$**:

With $\alpha = -\frac{9}{2}$ and $\beta = -4$, compute:

$ \beta - 2\alpha = -4 - 2\left(-\frac{9}{2}\right) = -4 + 9 = 5 $

Therefore, the difference $\beta - 2\alpha$ is 5.

**Correct option:** A
