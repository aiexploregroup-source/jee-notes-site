Watch a long jumper land: metres of flight ended by centimetres of sand. The pit’s job is brutal — kill all that kinetic energy over a tiny stopping distance, which is why the force on landing is enormous. The shorter the stop, the bigger the force.

Today’s physics drops a 2 kg ball from 10 m into sand that stops it within 10 cm — compute the average force, and respect the answer you get. Day 2 of Bonds & Binomials: chemistry draws the ion shapes hiding inside bromine trifluoride’s self-ionisation, and maths extracts a coefficient from an expansion.

Physics

## Work, Energy and Power

JEE Main 2026
Apr 2, Shift 2

A spherical ball of mass 2 kg falls from a height of 10 m and is brought to rest after penetrating 10 cm into sand. The average force exerted by sand on the ball is __________ N. (Take $g = 10\,\text{m/s}^2$)

- **A.** $1980$

- **B.** $2020$

- **C.** $2000$

- **D.** $1000$

**Solution / Answer**

Data: $m = 2\ \text{kg}$, $h = 10\ \text{m}$, penetration $d = 10\ \text{cm} = 0.1\ \text{m}$.

The ball starts and ends at rest, so total kinetic energy change is zero. Over the whole fall-plus-penetration, gravity acts through $(h + d)$ while the average sand force $F$ acts through $d$ opposing motion:

$$mg(h + d) - F\,d = 0$$

**Solve for $F$:**

$$F = \frac{mg(h + d)}{d} = \frac{(2)(10)(10 + 0.1)}{0.1} = \frac{202}{0.1} = 2020\ \text{N}$$

**Answer:** B ($2020$)

**Correct option:** B

Chemistry

## Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure

JEE Main 2026
8 Apr, Shift 2

Bromine trifluoride autoionizes to form $BrF_2^{\oplus}$ and $BrF_4^{\ominus}$. The shapes of the cation and anion are respectively __________, and __________.

- **A.** bent, square planar

- **B.** linear, square planar

- **C.** bent, see-saw

- **D.** linear, tetrahedral

**Solution / Answer**

Autoionization: $\ 2\,BrF_3 \rightleftharpoons BrF_2^{+} + BrF_4^{-}$.

**Cation $BrF_2^{+}$:** Br has $7$ electrons, $-1$ for the positive charge, $2$ used in bonding:

$$\text{lone pairs} = \frac{7 - 1 - 2}{2} = 2,\quad SN = 2 + 2 = 4\ (AX_2E_2)\ \Rightarrow\ \textbf{bent}$$

**Anion $BrF_4^{-}$:** Br has $7$ electrons, $+1$ for the negative charge, $4$ used in bonding:

$$\text{lone pairs} = \frac{7 + 1 - 4}{2} = 2,\quad SN = 4 + 2 = 6\ (AX_4E_2)\ \Rightarrow\ \textbf{square planar}$$

**Answer:** A

**Correct option:** A

Mathematics

## Binomial Theorem

JEE Main 2026
5 Apr, Shift 2

The coefficient of $x^2$ in the expansion of $\left(2x^2 + \dfrac{1}{x}\right)^{10}$, $x \neq 0$, is:

- **A.** 3240

- **B.** 3360

- **C.** 3480

- **D.** 3600

**Solution / Answer**

General term:

$$T_{r+1} = \binom{10}{r}(2x^2)^{10-r}\left(x^{-1}\right)^{r} = \binom{10}{r}\,2^{10-r}\,x^{20 - 3r}.$$

For $x^2$: $20 - 3r = 2 \Rightarrow r = 6.$

Coefficient:

$$\binom{10}{6}\,2^{4} = 210 \times 16 = 3360.$$

**Answer:** B (3360)

**Correct option:** B
