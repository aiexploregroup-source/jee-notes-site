PYQ · 2026 Apr 4, Shift 1

At $T\,(\text{K})$, the equilibrium constant of $\text{A}_2(g) + \text{B}_2(g) \rightleftharpoons \text{C}(g)$ is $2.7 \times 10^{-5}$. What is the equilibrium constant for $\frac{1}{3}\text{A}_2(g) + \frac{1}{3}\text{B}_2(g) \rightleftharpoons \frac{1}{3}\text{C}(g)$ at the same temperature?

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The target reaction is the original reaction multiplied by $\frac{1}{3}$. When a balanced equation is scaled by a factor $n$, the new equilibrium constant is $K^{n}$.</p>$$K' = K^{1/3} = \left(2.7 \times 10^{-5}\right)^{1/3}$$<p>Write $2.7 \times 10^{-5} = 27 \times 10^{-6}$:</p>$$K' = \left(27 \times 10^{-6}\right)^{1/3} = 27^{1/3} \times \left(10^{-6}\right)^{1/3} = 3 \times 10^{-2}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> D ($3 \times 10^{-2}$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 4, Shift 1

The pH of a solution obtained by mixing 5 mL of 0.1 M $\text{NH}_4\text{OH}$ solution with 250 mL of 0.1 M $\text{NH}_4\text{Cl}$ solution is _____ $\times 10^{-2}$. (Nearest integer)

Given: $\text{p}K_b\ (\text{NH}_4\text{OH}) = 4.74$; $\log 2 = 0.30$; $\log 3 = 0.48$; $\log 5 = 0.70$

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>This is a basic buffer (weak base $\text{NH}_4\text{OH}$ + its salt $\text{NH}_4\text{Cl}$).</p><p><strong>Millimoles:</strong></p>$$\text{base} = 5 \times 0.1 = 0.5\ \text{mmol}, \qquad \text{salt} = 250 \times 0.1 = 25\ \text{mmol}$$<p><strong>Henderson equation (basic form):</strong></p>$$\text{pOH} = \text{p}K_b + \log\frac{[\text{salt}]}{[\text{base}]} = 4.74 + \log\frac{25}{0.5} = 4.74 + \log 50$$

$$\log 50 = \log\frac{100}{2} = 2 - 0.30 = 1.70$$

$$\text{pOH} = 4.74 + 1.70 = 6.44 \implies \text{pH} = 14 - 6.44 = 7.56$$

$$\text{pH} = 7.56 = 756 \times 10^{-2}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 756</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 6, Shift 1

One mole each of He and A(g) are taken in a 10 L closed flask and heated to 400 K to establish the following equilibrium: $A(g) \rightleftharpoons B(g)$. $K_c$ for this reaction at 400 K is 4.0. The partial pressures (in atm) of He and B(g) respectively at equilibrium are (assume He, A(g) and B(g) behave as ideal gases; $R = 0.082\ \text{L atm K}^{-1}\text{mol}^{-1}$):

$$K_c = \frac{[B]}{[A]} = \frac{x}{1-x} = 4 \implies x = 0.8\ \text{mol}$$<p><strong>Moles at equilibrium:</strong> He $= 1$, B $= 0.8$.</p><p><strong>Partial pressures</strong> from $p = \dfrac{nRT}{V}$ with $\dfrac{RT}{V} = \dfrac{0.082 \times 400}{10} = 3.28$ atm/mol:</p>$$p_{\text{He}} = 1 \times 3.28 = 3.28\ \text{atm}$$<p></p>$$p_{B} = 0.8 \times 3.28 = 2.624\ \text{atm}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> A (3.28, 2.624)</p></div></details>

The solubility product constants of Ag$_2$CrO$_4$ and AgBr are $32x$ and $4y$ respectively at 298 K. The value of $\left(\dfrac{\text{molarity of Ag}_2\text{CrO}_4}{\text{molarity of AgBr}}\right)$ can be expressed as:

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Ag$_2$CrO$_4$</strong> ($\text{A}_2\text{B}$ type): $K_{sp} = 4s^3$.</p>$$32x = 4s_1^3 \implies s_1^3 = 8x \implies s_1 = (8x)^{1/3} = 2\,x^{1/3}$$<p><strong>AgBr</strong> ($\text{AB}$ type): $K_{sp} = s^2$.</p>$$4y = s_2^2 \implies s_2 = \sqrt{4y} = 2\sqrt{y}$$<p><strong>Ratio:</strong></p>$$\frac{s_1}{s_2} = \frac{2\,x^{1/3}}{2\sqrt{y}} = \frac{\sqrt[3]{x}}{\sqrt{y}}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> D $\left(\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{x}}{\sqrt{y}}\right)$</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 4, Shift 2

20 mL of a solution of acetic acid required 28.4 mL of 0.1 M NaOH for its neutralization. A solution (X) was prepared by mixing 20 mL of the above acetic acid and 14.2 mL of 0.1 M NaOH solution. What is the pH of the solution (X)? (p$K_a$ value of acetic acid is 4.75)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Find moles of acid</strong> from the full neutralization:</p>$$n_{\text{acid}} = 28.4 \times 0.1 = 2.84\ \text{mmol}$$<p><strong>In solution (X):</strong> NaOH added $= 14.2 \times 0.1 = 1.42\ \text{mmol}$.</p><p>This is exactly <strong>half</strong> of the acid ($1.42 = \tfrac{1}{2}\times 2.84$), converting half the acid to acetate:</p>$$\text{salt} = 1.42\ \text{mmol}, \qquad \text{acid left} = 2.84 - 1.42 = 1.42\ \text{mmol}$$<p><strong>Henderson equation</strong> — at the half-equivalence point $[\text{salt}] = [\text{acid}]$:</p>$$\text{pH} = \text{p}K_a + \log\frac{[\text{salt}]}{[\text{acid}]} = 4.75 + \log 1 = 4.75$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B (4.75)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 4, Shift 2

For the following reaction at 50 °C and 2 atm pressure, $2\mathrm{N_2O_5(g)} \rightleftharpoons 2\mathrm{N_2O_4(g)} + \mathrm{O_2(g)}$, N$_2$O$_5$ is 50% dissociated. The magnitude of standard free energy change at this temperature is $x$. $x =$ __________ J mol$^{-1}$ [Nearest integer].

Given: $R = 8.314\ \text{J mol}^{-1}\text{K}^{-1}$, $\log 2 = 0.30$, $\log 3 = 0.48$, $\ln 10 = 2.303$, $°C + 273 = K$

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Take 2 mol N$_2$O$_5$ initially; 50% dissociates $\Rightarrow$ 1 mol reacts.</p><table><thead><tr><th>Species</th><th>N$_2$O$_5$</th><th>N$_2$O$_4$</th><th>O$_2$</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>mol at eqm</td><td>$1$</td><td>$1$</td><td>$0.5$</td></tr></tbody></table><p>Total moles $= 2.5$. With $P = 2$ atm, partial pressures $p_i = x_i P$:</p>$$p_{\text{N}_2\text{O}_5} = \frac{1}{2.5}(2) = 0.8, \quad p_{\text{N}_2\text{O}_4} = 0.8, \quad p_{\text{O}_2} = \frac{0.5}{2.5}(2) = 0.4$$

$$K_p = \frac{p_{\text{N}_2\text{O}_4}^2\, p_{\text{O}_2}}{p_{\text{N}_2\text{O}_5}^2} = \frac{(0.8)^2 (0.4)}{(0.8)^2} = 0.4$$

$$\log K_p = \log\frac{4}{10} = 2\log 2 - 1 = 0.60 - 1 = -0.40$$

$$\Delta G^\circ = -2.303\,RT \log K_p = -2.303 \times 8.314 \times 323 \times (-0.40) \approx 2474\ \text{J mol}^{-1}$$

$$T = 50 + 273 = 323\ \text{K}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 2474</p></div></details>

The first and second ionization constants of a weak dibasic acid $H_2A$ are $8.1 \times 10^{-8}$ and $1.0 \times 10^{-13}$ respectively. 0.1 mol of $H_2A$ was dissolved in 1 L of 0.1 M HCl solution. The concentration of $HA^-$ in the resultant solution is:

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The strong acid HCl fixes $[\text{H}^+] \approx 0.1\ \text{M}$, which suppresses the (already very weak) ionization of $H_2A$, so $[H_2A] \approx 0.1\ \text{M}$.</p><p><strong>First ionization:</strong> $H_2A \rightleftharpoons H^+ + HA^-$</p>$$K_{a1} = \frac{[H^+][HA^-]}{[H_2A]}$$<p>Solve for $[HA^-]$:</p>$$[HA^-] = \frac{K_{a1}\,[H_2A]}{[H^+]} = \frac{(8.1 \times 10^{-8})(0.1)}{0.1} = 8.1 \times 10^{-8}\ \text{M}$$<p>(The second ionization, $K_{a2} = 10^{-13}$, is negligible.)</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> C ($8.1 \times 10^{-8}\,\text{M}$)</p></div></details>

At $25\,°C$, 20.0 mL of 0.2 M weak monoprotic acid HX is titrated against 0.2 M NaOH. The pH of the solution (a) at the start of the titration (when NaOH has not been added) and (b) when 10 mL of NaOH is added respectively, are:

PYQ · 2026 Apr 6, Shift 2

Given is a concentrated solution of a weak electrolyte A$_x$B$_y$ of concentration ‘$c$’ and dissociation constant ‘$K$’. The degree of dissociation is given by:

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Dissociation: $\text{A}_x\text{B}_y \rightleftharpoons x\,\text{A}^{y+} + y\,\text{B}^{x-}$.</p><p>With degree of dissociation $\alpha$ (and $\alpha \ll 1$, so $[\text{A}_x\text{B}_y] \approx c$):</p>$$[\text{A}^{y+}] = xc\alpha, \qquad [\text{B}^{x-}] = yc\alpha$$

$$K = \frac{[\text{A}^{y+}]^x\,[\text{B}^{x-}]^y}{[\text{A}_x\text{B}_y]} = \frac{(xc\alpha)^x (yc\alpha)^y}{c} = x^x y^y\, c^{x+y-1}\, \alpha^{x+y}$$<p>Solve for $\alpha$:</p>$$\alpha^{x+y} = \frac{K}{c^{x+y-1}\,x^x y^y} \implies \alpha = \left(\frac{K}{c^{x+y-1}\,x^x y^y}\right)^{\frac{1}{x+y}}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B $\left(\left(\dfrac{K}{c^{x+y-1}x^x y^y}\right)^{\frac{1}{x+y}}\right)$</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 6, Shift 2

In a closed flask at 600 K, one mole of X$_2$Y$_4$(g) attains equilibrium as given below:

$$X_2Y_4(g)\rightleftharpoons 2XY_2(g)$$

At equilibrium, 75% X$_2$Y$_4$(g) was dissociated and the total pressure is 1 atm. The magnitude of $\Delta_rG^\ominus$ (in kJ mol$^{-1}$) at this temperature is __________. (Nearest Integer)

Given: $R = 8.3\ \text{J mol}^{-1}\text{K}^{-1}$; $\ln 10 = 2.3$, $\log 2 = 0.3$, $\log 3 = 0.48$, $\log 5 = 0.69$, $\log 7 = 0.84$

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Start with 1 mol X$_2$Y$_4$; $\alpha = 0.75$.</p><table><thead><tr><th>Species</th><th>X$_2$Y$_4$</th><th>XY$_2$</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>mol at eqm</td><td>$1-\alpha = 0.25$</td><td>$2\alpha = 1.5$</td></tr></tbody></table><p>Total moles $= 1 + \alpha = 1.75$. With $P = 1$ atm:</p>$$p_{X_2Y_4} = \frac{0.25}{1.75} = \frac{1}{7}, \qquad p_{XY_2} = \frac{1.5}{1.75} = \frac{6}{7}$$

$$K_p = \frac{p_{XY_2}^2}{p_{X_2Y_4}} = \frac{(6/7)^2}{1/7} = \frac{36/49}{1/7} = \frac{36}{7}$$

$$\log K_p = \log 36 - \log 7 = (2\log 2 + 2\log 3) - \log 7 = (0.60 + 0.96) - 0.84 = 0.72$$

$$\Delta_rG^\ominus = -2.3\,RT\log K_p = -2.3 \times 8.3 \times 600 \times 0.72 \approx -8247\ \text{J mol}^{-1} \approx -8.25\ \text{kJ mol}^{-1}$$<p>Magnitude $\approx 8\ \text{kJ mol}^{-1}$.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> 8</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 5, Shift 1

The values of the pressure equilibrium constant recorded at different temperatures for the following equilibrium reaction have been given below:

$$A(g) \rightleftharpoons B(g) + C(g)$$

| $\frac{1}{T}\,(K^{-1})$ | $\log_{10}K_p$ |
| --- | --- |
| 0.05 | 3.5 |
| 0.06 | 2.5 |
| 0.07 | 1.5 |

The magnitude of $\frac{\Delta H^\circ}{R}$ calculated from the above data is ________. (Nearest integer)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The van’t Hoff equation in log form:</p>$$\log_{10} K_p = -\frac{\Delta H^\circ}{2.303\,R}\cdot\frac{1}{T} + \text{constant}$$<p>So the plot of $\log_{10}K_p$ vs $\frac{1}{T}$ is a straight line of slope $-\dfrac{\Delta H^\circ}{2.303\,R}$.</p><p><strong>Slope</strong> from any two points:</p>$$\text{slope} = \frac{1.5 - 3.5}{0.07 - 0.05} = \frac{-2}{0.02} = -100$$<p>Therefore:</p>$$-\frac{\Delta H^\circ}{2.303\,R} = -100 \implies \frac{\Delta H^\circ}{R} = 100 \times 2.303 = 230.3$$<p>Magnitude (nearest integer) $= 230$.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> 230</p></div></details>

The reaction $A(g) \rightleftharpoons B(g) + C(g)$ was initiated with the amount ‘$a$’ of $A(g)$. At equilibrium it is found that the amount of $A(g)$ remaining is $(a - x)$ at a total pressure of $p$. The equilibrium constant $K_p$ of the reaction can be calculated from the expression:

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Moles at equilibrium:</strong></p><table><thead><tr><th>$A$</th><th>$B$</th><th>$C$</th><th>total</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>$a-x$</td><td>$x$</td><td>$x$</td><td>$a+x$</td></tr></tbody></table><p><strong>Partial pressures</strong> ($p_i = \text{mole fraction} \times p$):</p>$$p_A = \frac{a-x}{a+x}\,p, \qquad p_B = p_C = \frac{x}{a+x}\,p$$

$$K_p = \frac{p_B\,p_C}{p_A} = \frac{\left(\dfrac{x}{a+x}\,p\right)^2}{\dfrac{a-x}{a+x}\,p} = \frac{x^2\,p}{(a+x)(a-x)} = \frac{x^2}{a^2 - x^2}\times p$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B $\left(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2 - x^2} \times p\right)$</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 8, Shift 2

Consider the following reactions in which all the reactants and products are in the gaseous state:

$$2xy \rightleftharpoons x_2 + y_2 \qquad K_1 = 2.5 \times 10^5$$

$$xy + \tfrac{1}{2}z_2 \rightleftharpoons xyz \qquad K_2 = 5 \times 10^{-3}$$

The value of $K_3$ for the equilibrium $\frac{1}{2}x_2 + \frac{1}{2}y_2 + \frac{1}{2}z_2 \rightleftharpoons xyz$ is:

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Build the target reaction from the given ones.</p><p><strong>Reverse and halve reaction 1:</strong> $\ \frac{1}{2}x_2 + \frac{1}{2}y_2 \rightleftharpoons xy$, with $K = \left(\dfrac{1}{K_1}\right)^{1/2}$.</p><p><strong>Add reaction 2:</strong> $\ xy + \frac{1}{2}z_2 \rightleftharpoons xyz$, with $K_2$.</p><p>Adding these gives the target, so multiply the constants:</p>$$K_3 = \left(\frac{1}{K_1}\right)^{1/2}\times K_2 = \left(\frac{1}{2.5 \times 10^5}\right)^{1/2}\times (5 \times 10^{-3})$$

$$\left(\frac{1}{2.5 \times 10^5}\right)^{1/2} = \left(4 \times 10^{-6}\right)^{1/2} = 2 \times 10^{-3}$$

$$K_3 = (2 \times 10^{-3})(5 \times 10^{-3}) = 1.0 \times 10^{-5}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> C ($1.0 \times 10^{-5}$)</p></div></details>

$$C(s) + CO_2(g) \rightleftharpoons 2CO(g) \qquad K_{p_2} = 2\ \text{atm}$$
