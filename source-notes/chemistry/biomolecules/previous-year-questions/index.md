PYQ · 2026 4 Apr, Shift 2

A D-aldotetrose on oxidation with concentrated $\text{HNO}_3$ gives an **optically inactive** dicarboxylic acid. Identify the Fischer projection of the D-aldotetrose from the options.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>An aldotetrose is $\text{CHO}-\text{CHOH}-\text{CHOH}-\text{CH}_2\text{OH}$. Oxidation with conc. $\text{HNO}_3$ converts <strong>both</strong> the terminal $\text{CHO}$ and $\text{CH}_2\text{OH}$ into $-\text{COOH}$, giving a tartaric acid (2,3-dihydroxybutanedioic acid).</p><p><strong>D-series requirement:</strong> the reference carbon C3 (lowest chiral centre) must have its $\text{OH}$ on the <strong>right</strong>. This rules out any structure with C3-$\text{OH}$ on the left.</p><p>The two D-aldotetroses are:</p><ul><li><strong>D-erythrose</strong> — C2-$\text{OH}$ right, C3-$\text{OH}$ right (both same side).</li><li><strong>D-threose</strong> — C2-$\text{OH}$ left, C3-$\text{OH}$ right (opposite sides).</li></ul>$$\text{D-erythrose} \xrightarrow{\text{HNO}_3} \textbf{meso-tartaric acid (optically inactive)}$$<p>The two $\text{OH}$ groups end up on the same side, giving a molecule with an internal plane of symmetry $\Rightarrow$ optically <strong>inactive</strong>. D-threose instead gives an optically active tartaric acid.</p><p>Therefore the required structure has <strong>C2-$\text{OH}$ right and C3-$\text{OH}$ right</strong> (D-erythrose), which is option C.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> C</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 5 Apr, Shift 1

Identify the correct statements.

A. Glucose exists in two anomeric forms.

B. Anomers of glucose differ in configuration at C-1 in the cyclic hemiacetal structure.

C. Melting point of the $\alpha$-anomer of glucose is greater than the $\beta$-anomer.

D. Specific rotation of the $\alpha$-anomer is $+19^\circ$ while that of the $\beta$-anomer is $+112^\circ$.

E. $\alpha$- and $\beta$-anomers of glucose are prepared by crystallization of a saturated glucose solution at $303\ \text{K}$ and $371\ \text{K}$ respectively.

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Evaluate each statement:</p><ul><li><strong>A — True.</strong> Glucose exists as $\alpha$- and $\beta$-anomers.</li><li><strong>B — True.</strong> The anomers differ only in configuration at the anomeric carbon C-1 of the cyclic hemiacetal.</li><li><strong>C — False.</strong> $\alpha$-D-glucose melts at $\approx 146\,^\circ\text{C}$ and $\beta$-D-glucose at $\approx 150\,^\circ\text{C}$, so $\alpha &lt; \beta$.</li><li><strong>D — False.</strong> The specific rotations are <strong>swapped</strong>: $\alpha$-anomer $= +111^\circ$ (≈ $+112^\circ$) and $\beta$-anomer $= +19^\circ$.</li><li><strong>E — True.</strong> $\alpha$-glucose crystallises from saturated solution at $303\ \text{K}$ and $\beta$-glucose above $371\ \text{K}$.</li></ul><p>Correct statements: <strong>A, B and E</strong>.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> D</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 8 Apr, Shift 2

Which of the following amino acids will give a **violet coloured** complex with neutral ferric chloride solution?

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>A violet/coloured complex with <strong>neutral $\text{FeCl}_3$</strong> is characteristic of a <strong>phenolic $-\text{OH}$</strong> group.</p><ul><li><strong>Threonine, Serine</strong> — bear aliphatic ($-\text{CHOH}$ / $-\text{CH}_2\text{OH}$) hydroxyls, no colour.</li><li><strong>Cysteine</strong> — bears a thiol ($-\text{SH}$), no violet colour.</li><li><strong>Tyrosine</strong> — bears a <strong>phenol</strong> ring $\Rightarrow$ gives the violet Fe–phenolate complex.</li></ul><p><strong>Answer:</strong> C</p></div></details>
