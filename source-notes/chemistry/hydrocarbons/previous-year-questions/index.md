PYQ · 2026 4 Apr, Shift 1

Consider the sequence of reactions on benzene:

$$\text{Benzene} \xrightarrow[\text{(iii) K}_2\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_7/\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4]{\text{(i) CH}_3\text{Cl}/\text{anhy. AlCl}_3\quad \text{(ii) Cl}_2/\text{FeCl}_3} (X)$$

$P$ g of the major product $(X)$ is treated with $\text{NaHCO}_3$ solution, liberating a gas that occupies $11.2\ \text{dm}^3$ at STP. Find $P$. (Molar masses: H = 1, C = 12, O = 16, Cl = 35.5)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Trace the sequence:</p><ul><li><strong>(i)</strong> Friedel-Crafts methylation gives <strong>toluene</strong> ($\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CH}_3$).</li><li><strong>(ii)</strong> Ring chlorination (the $-\text{CH}_3$ is o/p-directing) gives <strong>chlorotoluene</strong>.</li><li><strong>(iii)</strong> $\text{K}_2\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_7/\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ oxidises the $-\text{CH}_3$ side chain to $-\text{COOH}$.</li></ul><p>So $(X)$ is <strong>chlorobenzoic acid</strong>, $\text{Cl-C}_6\text{H}_4\text{-COOH}$ (formula $\text{C}_7\text{H}_5\text{ClO}_2$).</p><p>Only the $-\text{COOH}$ group reacts with $\text{NaHCO}_3$, releasing $\text{CO}_2$:</p>$$\text{Ar-COOH} + \text{NaHCO}_3 \rightarrow \text{Ar-COONa} + \text{H}_2\text{O} + \text{CO}_2\uparrow$$<p>Moles of gas:</p>$$n_{\text{CO}_2} = \dfrac{11.2}{22.4} = 0.5\ \text{mol}$$<p>Since $1\ \text{mol}$ of acid $\to 1\ \text{mol}\ \text{CO}_2$, moles of $(X) = 0.5$.</p><p>Molar mass of $\text{C}_7\text{H}_5\text{ClO}_2$:</p>$$7(12) + 5(1) + 35.5 + 2(16) = 84 + 5 + 35.5 + 32 = 156.5\ \text{g mol}^{-1}$$

$$P = 0.5 \times 156.5 = 78.25\ \text{g}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 78.25</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 6 Apr, Shift 1

**Statement I:** Methane can be prepared by decarboxylation of sodium ethanoate, Kolbe’s electrolysis of sodium acetate, and reaction of $\text{CH}_3\text{MgBr}$ with water.

**Statement II:** Methane cannot be prepared from unsaturated hydrocarbons and by the Wurtz reaction.

In light of the above, choose the correct answer.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Check Statement I:</strong></p><ul><li>Decarboxylation (soda-lime): $\text{CH}_3\text{COONa} + \text{NaOH} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \text{CH}_4 + \text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3$ — gives methane. ✓</li><li>$\text{CH}_3\text{MgBr} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{CH}_4 + \text{Mg(OH)Br}$ — gives methane. ✓</li><li><strong>Kolbe’s electrolysis</strong> of sodium acetate: $2\,\text{CH}_3\text{COO}^- \rightarrow \text{CH}_3\text{-CH}_3 + 2\text{CO}_2 + 2e^-$ — gives <strong>ethane, not methane</strong>. ✗</li></ul><p>So Statement I is <strong>false</strong>.</p><p><strong>Check Statement II:</strong></p><ul><li>Wurtz reaction couples two alkyl halides, so the smallest possible product is ethane; it <strong>cannot</strong> give methane.</li><li>Hydrogenation of unsaturated hydrocarbons (e.g. $\text{CH}_2\text{=CH}_2 \to \text{C}_2\text{H}_6$) also cannot yield methane.</li></ul><p>So Statement II is <strong>true</strong>.</p><p>Statement I false, Statement II true.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> D</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 1

Consider the isomers of the hydrocarbon $\text{C}_5\text{H}_{10}$ that do **not** decolourise $\text{KMnO}_4$ solution. These isomers undergo chlorination with $\text{Cl}_2$ in the presence of light to give monochloro compounds. The total number of monochloro compounds (structural isomers only) formed is _____.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Isomers of $\text{C}_5\text{H}_{10}$ that <strong>do not</strong> decolourise $\text{KMnO}_4$ are the saturated <strong>cycloalkanes</strong> (no C=C):</p><ol><li><strong>Cyclopentane</strong> — all 10 H equivalent $\Rightarrow$ <strong>1</strong> product.</li><li><strong>Methylcyclobutane</strong> — distinct positions: $-\text{CH}_3$, C1 (ring C bearing methyl), C2/C4 (equivalent), C3 $\Rightarrow$ <strong>4</strong> products.</li><li><strong>Ethylcyclopropane</strong> — distinct positions: terminal $\text{CH}_3$, ethyl $\text{CH}_2$, ring C1, ring C2/C3 (equivalent) $\Rightarrow$ <strong>4</strong> products.</li><li><strong>1,1-Dimethylcyclopropane</strong> — distinct positions: $\text{CH}_3$ (both equivalent), ring $\text{CH}_2$ (C2/C3 equivalent) $\Rightarrow$ <strong>2</strong> products.</li><li><strong>1,2-Dimethylcyclopropane</strong> (one structural isomer; cis/trans are stereoisomers) — distinct positions: $\text{CH}_3$ (equivalent), ring CH bearing methyl (C1/C2 equivalent), ring $\text{CH}_2$ (C3) $\Rightarrow$ <strong>3</strong> products.</li></ol><p>Total:</p>$$1 + 4 + 4 + 2 + 3 = 14$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 14</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 1

One mole of an alkane $(x)$ requires $8$ moles of oxygen for complete combustion. The sum of the number of carbon and hydrogen atoms in the alkane $(x)$ is _____.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>General combustion of an alkane $\text{C}_n\text{H}_{2n+2}$:</p>$$\text{C}_n\text{H}_{2n+2} + \left(\dfrac{3n+1}{2}\right)\text{O}_2 \rightarrow n\,\text{CO}_2 + (n+1)\,\text{H}_2\text{O}$$<p>Set the $\text{O}_2$ requirement equal to 8:</p>$$\dfrac{3n+1}{2} = 8 \;\Rightarrow\; 3n + 1 = 16 \;\Rightarrow\; n = 5$$<p>So the alkane is $\text{C}_5\text{H}_{12}$ (pentane).</p>$$\text{Carbon atoms} + \text{Hydrogen atoms} = 5 + 12 = 17$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 17</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 4 Apr, Shift 2

Styrene $(X)$ ($\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{-CH=CH}_2$) undergoes the following sequence:

(i) $\text{Br}_2/\text{CHCl}_3$; (ii) $\text{NaNH}_2$ (excess); (iii) $\text{CH}_3\text{I}$; (iv) $\text{H}_2,\ \text{Na}/\text{NH}_3(l)$ $\rightarrow$ major product $(Y)$.

The molar mass of $(Y)$ is _____ g mol$^{-1}$. (C = 12, H = 1, O = 16)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Track the transformation:</p><ul><li><strong>(i)</strong> $\text{Br}_2$ adds across the double bond: $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{-CHBr-CH}_2\text{Br}$ (a vicinal dibromide).</li><li><strong>(ii)</strong> Excess $\text{NaNH}_2$ performs double dehydrohalogenation to give the terminal alkyne <strong>phenylacetylene</strong>, $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{-C}{\equiv}\text{CH}$ (and deprotonates it to the acetylide).</li><li><strong>(iii)</strong> The acetylide is alkylated by $\text{CH}_3\text{I}$: $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{-C}{\equiv}\text{C-CH}_3$ (1-phenylpropyne, $\text{C}_9\text{H}_8$).</li><li><strong>(iv)</strong> $\text{Na}/\text{NH}_3(l)$ (dissolving-metal reduction) reduces the internal alkyne to the <strong>trans-alkene</strong>: $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{-CH=CH-CH}_3$ ($\text{C}_9\text{H}_{10}$).</li></ul><p>Molar mass of $(Y) = \text{C}_9\text{H}_{10}$:</p>$$9(12) + 10(1) = 108 + 10 = 118\ \text{g mol}^{-1}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 2

The compound $(X)$:
(i) on heating with anhydrous $\text{AlCl}_3$ and HCl gas gives 2,4-dimethylpentane,
(ii) on aromatization gives toluene, and
(iii) on cyclisation gives methylcyclohexane.

The correct name of $(X)$ is:

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>All three products contain <strong>7 carbon atoms</strong>, so $(X)$ is a $\text{C}_7$ hydrocarbon:</p><ul><li>2,4-dimethylpentane is $\text{C}_7\text{H}_{16}$ — a <strong>skeletal isomer</strong> of heptane (formed by $\text{AlCl}_3$-catalysed isomerisation of an alkane).</li><li>Aromatization (dehydrogenation) to toluene ($\text{C}_7\text{H}_8$) and cyclisation to methylcyclohexane ($\text{C}_7\text{H}_{14}$) are the classic <strong>catalytic reforming</strong> reactions of a straight-chain alkane.</li></ul><p>Only a <strong>saturated straight-chain $\text{C}_7$ alkane, n-heptane</strong>, undergoes all three (isomerisation, aromatization, cyclisation). The triene/alkene options are unsaturated and would not give the saturated 2,4-dimethylpentane by mere $\text{AlCl}_3$ isomerisation.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> C</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 6 Apr, Shift 2

An alkane $(Y)$ requires $8$ moles of oxygen for complete combustion, and on chlorination with $\text{Cl}_2/h\nu$ gives only **one** monochlorinated product $(Z)$. The total number of primary carbon atoms in $(Y)$ is _____.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Find the molecular formula.</strong> From $\dfrac{3n+1}{2} = 8$, we get $n = 5$, so $(Y)$ is $\text{C}_5\text{H}_{12}$.</p><p><strong>Use the “only one monochloro product” clue.</strong> Among the three $\text{C}_5\text{H}_{12}$ isomers:</p><ul><li>n-pentane $\to$ 3 monochloro products,</li><li>2-methylbutane $\to$ 4 monochloro products,</li><li><strong>2,2-dimethylpropane (neopentane), $\text{C(CH}_3)_4$</strong> $\to$ all 12 H are equivalent $\to$ <strong>only 1</strong> monochloro product. ✓</li></ul><p>So $(Y)$ is neopentane, $\text{C(CH}_3)_4$. Its four $\text{CH}_3$ groups are all <strong>primary carbons</strong> (the central carbon is quaternary).</p>$$\text{Primary carbon atoms} = 4$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 4</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 5 Apr, Shift 2

The IUPAC names of some alkenes are given below. Find the correct **stability** order.

A. 2-Methylbut-2-ene B. cis-But-2-ene C. 2,3-Dimethylbut-2-ene D. Prop-1-ene

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Alkene stability increases with the <strong>number of alkyl groups on the double-bonded carbons</strong> (hyperconjugation and $+I$ effect):</p><ul><li><strong>C. 2,3-Dimethylbut-2-ene</strong>, $(\text{CH}_3)_2\text{C=C(CH}_3)_2$ — <strong>tetrasubstituted</strong> (most stable).</li><li><strong>A. 2-Methylbut-2-ene</strong>, $(\text{CH}_3)_2\text{C=CHCH}_3$ — <strong>trisubstituted</strong>.</li><li><strong>B. cis-But-2-ene</strong>, $\text{CH}_3\text{CH=CHCH}_3$ — <strong>disubstituted</strong>.</li><li><strong>D. Prop-1-ene</strong>, $\text{CH}_3\text{CH=CH}_2$ — <strong>monosubstituted</strong> (least stable).</li></ul><p>Order of stability:</p>$$\text{C} &gt; \text{A} &gt; \text{B} &gt; \text{D}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> A</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 5 Apr, Shift 2

Identify the correct IUPAC name of the hydrocarbon $(x)$ that contains **three primary carbon atoms** and has a **molar mass of 72 g mol$^{-1}$**.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Molar mass filter.</strong> $M = 72\ \text{g mol}^{-1}$ corresponds to $\text{C}_5\text{H}_{12}$ ($5\times12 + 12 = 72$).</p><ul><li>1,1-Dimethylcyclopropane is $\text{C}_5\text{H}_{10}$ ($M = 70$) — <strong>rejected</strong>.</li></ul><p><strong>Primary-carbon filter</strong> (a primary carbon is a $\text{CH}_3$ bonded to only one other carbon):</p><ul><li><strong>2,2-Dimethylpropane</strong>, $\text{C(CH}_3)_4$ — <strong>4</strong> primary carbons. ✗</li><li><strong>2-Methylbutane</strong>, $(\text{CH}_3)_2\text{CH-CH}_2\text{-CH}_3$ — the two methyls on C2 plus the terminal $\text{CH}_3$ give <strong>3</strong> primary carbons. ✓</li><li><strong>n-pentane</strong>, $\text{CH}_3(\text{CH}_2)_3\text{CH}_3$ — only <strong>2</strong> primary carbons. ✗</li></ul><p>Both conditions are satisfied only by <strong>2-methylbutane</strong>.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> C</p></div></details>

The gas is then reacted with iodine in the presence of $\text{HIO}_3$ to give compound $(X)$. Compound $(X)$ with Na in dry ether gives compound $(Y)$. The molar mass of $(Y)$ is _____ g mol$^{-1}$. (Nearest integer)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Identify the gas from its density at STP.</strong> Molar volume at STP is $22.4\ \text{dm}^3\text{/mol}$:</p>$$M = \dfrac{22.4\ \text{dm}^3/\text{mol}}{1.4\ \text{dm}^3/\text{g}} = 16\ \text{g mol}^{-1} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{gas is } \text{CH}_4$$<p>So $\text{R} = \text{CH}_3$ and $\text{RMgI} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{CH}_4 + \text{Mg(OH)I}$.</p><p><strong>Iodination.</strong> $\text{CH}_4 + \text{I}_2 \rightleftharpoons \text{CH}_3\text{I} + \text{HI}$. $\text{HIO}_3$ oxidises the $\text{HI}$ back to $\text{I}_2$, driving the reaction forward, so $(X) = \text{CH}_3\text{I}$.</p><p><strong>Wurtz reaction.</strong> $2\,\text{CH}_3\text{I} + 2\,\text{Na} \xrightarrow{\text{dry ether}} \text{CH}_3\text{-CH}_3 + 2\,\text{NaI}$, so $(Y)$ is <strong>ethane</strong>, $\text{C}_2\text{H}_6$.</p><p>Molar mass of $(Y)$:</p>$$2(12) + 6(1) = 30\ \text{g mol}^{-1}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 30</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 8 Apr, Shift 2

In which of the following reactions is the major product **not** obtained by a rearrangement reaction?

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Examine each pathway for a carbocation rearrangement:</p><ul><li><strong>A.</strong> Benzene + n-propyl chloride/$\text{AlCl}_3$: the 1° n-propyl cation rearranges by a <strong>1,2-hydride shift</strong> to the more stable 2° isopropyl cation, giving cumene. <strong>Rearrangement occurs.</strong></li><li><strong>B.</strong> tert-Butyl alcohol + $\text{H}_3\text{O}^+$: protonation and loss of water gives the <strong>3° tert-butyl cation directly</strong>, which simply eliminates a $\beta$-H to form 2-methylprop-1-ene. <strong>No rearrangement</strong> — the initial cation is already the most stable one.</li><li><strong>C.</strong> n-Hexane + $\text{AlCl}_3/\text{HCl}$: isomerisation to 2-methylpentane proceeds through hydride/methyl shifts. <strong>Rearrangement occurs.</strong></li><li><strong>D.</strong> Neopentyl alcohol + $\text{H}_3\text{O}^+$: the 1° neopentyl cation undergoes a <strong>1,2-methyl shift</strong> to a 3° cation, giving 2-methylbut-2-ene. <strong>Rearrangement occurs.</strong></li></ul><p>Only <strong>B</strong> gives its product without any rearrangement.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> B</p></div></details>
