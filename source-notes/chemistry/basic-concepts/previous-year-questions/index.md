PYQ · 2026 6 Apr, Shift 1

An oxide of iron contains $69.9\%$ iron. Its empirical formula is:

(Given: Molar mass of Fe $= 56\ \text{g mol}^{-1}$, O $= 16\ \text{g mol}^{-1}$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Step 1 — Mass of each element in 100 g:</strong></p><ul><li>Fe $= 69.9\ \text{g}$, O $= 100 - 69.9 = 30.1\ \text{g}$</li></ul><p><strong>Step 2 — Convert to moles:</strong></p>$$n_\text{Fe} = \frac{69.9}{56} \approx 1.248, \qquad n_\text{O} = \frac{30.1}{16} \approx 1.881$$<p><strong>Step 3 — Simplest ratio</strong> (divide by smallest):</p>$$\text{Fe} : \text{O} = \frac{1.248}{1.248} : \frac{1.881}{1.248} = 1 : 1.507 \approx 2 : 3$$<p>Empirical formula $= \text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3$ (check: %Fe $= \tfrac{112}{160} = 70.0\%$).</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> B</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 1

The mass of iron converted into $\text{Fe}_3\text{O}_4$ by the action of $18\ \text{g}$ of steam is:

(Given: Molar mass of H, O and Fe are $1$, $16$ and $56\ \text{g mol}^{-1}$ respectively. Assume iron is present in excess.)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Reaction:</strong></p>$$3\,\text{Fe} + 4\,\text{H}_2\text{O} \longrightarrow \text{Fe}_3\text{O}_4 + 4\,\text{H}_2$$<p><strong>Step 1 — Moles of steam:</strong></p>$$n_{\text{H}_2\text{O}} = \frac{18}{18} = 1\ \text{mol}$$<p><strong>Step 2 — Moles of Fe</strong> (ratio $3:4$ with water):</p>$$n_{\text{Fe}} = \frac{3}{4} \times 1 = 0.75\ \text{mol}$$<p><strong>Step 3 — Mass of Fe:</strong></p>$$m_{\text{Fe}} = 0.75 \times 56 = 42\ \text{g}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> D</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 4 Apr, Shift 2

The correct order of total number of atoms present in
(A) $2$ moles of cyclohexane,
(B) $684\ \text{g}$ of sucrose,
(C) $90.8\ \text{L}$ of dihydrogen at STP
is:

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Express each total atom count in units of $N_A$.</p><p><strong>(A) Cyclohexane</strong> $\text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}$: $18$ atoms per molecule.</p>$$2 \times 18 = 36$$<p><strong>(B) Sucrose</strong> $\text{C}_{12}\text{H}_{22}\text{O}_{11}$, $M = 342$: $45$ atoms per molecule.</p>$$n = \frac{684}{342} = 2\ \text{mol} \Rightarrow 2 \times 45 = 90$$<p><strong>(C) Dihydrogen</strong> at STP ($V_m = 22.7\ \text{L mol}^{-1}$): $2$ atoms per molecule.</p>$$n = \frac{90.8}{22.7} = 4\ \text{mol} \Rightarrow 4 \times 2 = 8$$<p>Order: $B(90) &gt; A(36) &gt; C(8)$, i.e. $B &gt; A &gt; C$.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> D</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 2

The ratio of mass percentage (w/w) of C : H in a hydrocarbon is $12 : 1$. It has two carbon atoms. The weight (in g) of $\text{CO}_2(g)$ formed when $3.38\ \text{g}$ of this hydrocarbon is completely burnt in oxygen is:

(Given: Molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ — C : $12$, H : $1$, O : $16$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Step 1 — Mole ratio of atoms</strong> from mass ratio $12:1$:</p>$$\text{C} : \text{H} = \frac{12}{12} : \frac{1}{1} = 1 : 1$$<p>Empirical formula $\text{CH}$; with two carbons the molecule is $\text{C}_2\text{H}_2$, $M = 26$.</p><p><strong>Step 2 — Moles of hydrocarbon:</strong></p>$$n = \frac{3.38}{26} = 0.13\ \text{mol}$$<p><strong>Step 3 — Moles of $\text{CO}_2$</strong> ($2$ carbons per molecule):</p>$$n_{\text{CO}_2} = 2 \times 0.13 = 0.26\ \text{mol}$$<p><strong>Step 4 — Mass:</strong></p>$$m_{\text{CO}_2} = 0.26 \times 44 = 11.44\ \text{g}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 6 Apr, Shift 2

Which of the following contain the same number of atoms?
(Given: Molar mass in g mol$^{-1}$ of H, He, O and S are $1$, $4$, $16$ and $32$ respectively)
A. $2\ \text{g}$ of $\text{O}_2$ gas
B. $4\ \text{g}$ of $\text{SO}_2$ gas
C. $1400\ \text{mL}$ of $\text{O}_2$ at STP
D. $0.05\ \text{L}$ of He at STP
E. $0.0625\ \text{mol}$ of $\text{H}_2$ gas

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Total atoms (in mol) $=$ moles $\times$ atoms per molecule, with $V_m = 22.4\ \text{L mol}^{-1}$.</p>$$\text{A: } \frac{2}{32}\times 2 = 0.0625 \times 2 = 0.125$$

$$\text{B: } \frac{4}{64}\times 3 = 0.0625 \times 3 = 0.1875$$

$$\text{C: } \frac{1.4}{22.4}\times 2 = 0.0625 \times 2 = 0.125$$

$$\text{D: } \frac{0.05}{22.4}\times 1 = 0.00223$$

$$\text{E: } 0.0625 \times 2 = 0.125$$<p>A, C and E each give $0.125$ mol of atoms — the same number.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> D</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 5 Apr, Shift 1

How many grams of residue is obtained by heating $2.76\ \text{g}$ of silver carbonate?

(Given: Molar mass of C, O and Ag are $12$, $16$ and $108\ \text{g mol}^{-1}$ respectively)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Reactions</strong> — the final residue is metallic silver:</p>$$\text{Ag}_2\text{CO}_3 \xrightarrow{\Delta} \text{Ag}_2\text{O} + \text{CO}_2, \qquad 2\,\text{Ag}_2\text{O} \xrightarrow{\Delta} 4\,\text{Ag} + \text{O}_2$$<p><strong>Step 1 — Molar mass of $\text{Ag}_2\text{CO}_3$:</strong></p>$$2(108) + 12 + 3(16) = 276\ \text{g mol}^{-1}$$<p><strong>Step 2 — Moles:</strong></p>$$n = \frac{2.76}{276} = 0.01\ \text{mol}$$<p><strong>Step 3 — Mass of Ag</strong> ($2$ mol Ag per mol $\text{Ag}_2\text{CO}_3$):</p>$$m_{\text{Ag}} = (0.01 \times 2) \times 108 = 2.16\ \text{g}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 5 Apr, Shift 2

What volume of hydrogen gas at STP would be liberated by action of $50\ \text{mL}$ of $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$ of $50\%$ purity (density $= 1.3\ \text{g mL}^{-1}$) on $20\ \text{g}$ of zinc?

(Given: Molar mass of H, O, S, Zn are $1$, $16$, $32$, $65\ \text{g mol}^{-1}$ respectively.)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Reaction:</strong></p>$$\text{Zn} + \text{H}_2\text{SO}_4 \longrightarrow \text{ZnSO}_4 + \text{H}_2$$<p><strong>Step 1 — Moles of $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$:</strong> mass of solution $= 50 \times 1.3 = 65\ \text{g}$; pure acid $= 50\% = 32.5\ \text{g}$.</p>$$n_{\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4} = \frac{32.5}{98} = 0.332\ \text{mol}$$<p><strong>Step 2 — Moles of Zn:</strong></p>$$n_{\text{Zn}} = \frac{20}{65} = 0.308\ \text{mol}$$<p><strong>Step 3 — Limiting reagent:</strong> ratio $1:1$, so Zn limits ($0.308 &lt; 0.332$). Hence $n_{\text{H}_2} = 0.308\ \text{mol}$.</p><p><strong>Step 4 — Volume at STP</strong> ($V_m = 22.4\ \text{L mol}^{-1}$):</p>$$V_{\text{H}_2} = 0.308 \times 22.4 = 6.892\ \text{L}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> C</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 8 Apr, Shift 2

Match List - I with List - II.

**List - I (Mass of substance):**
A. $1.8\ \text{mg}$ water
B. $9.8\ \text{mg}$ sulphuric acid
C. $1.8\ \text{mg}$ carbon
D. $5.85\ \text{mg}$ salt (NaCl)

**List - II (Number of atoms):**
I. $2 \times 10^{-4} \times N_A$
II. $1.5 \times 10^{-4} \times N_A$
III. $3 \times 10^{-4} \times N_A$
IV. $7 \times 10^{-4} \times N_A$

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>For each, atoms $=$ moles $\times$ atoms per formula unit (in units of $N_A$).</p>$$\text{A. } \text{H}_2\text{O}\ (M=18): \frac{1.8\times 10^{-3}}{18}\times 3 = 3\times 10^{-4}\,N_A \to \text{III}$$

$$\text{B. } \text{H}_2\text{SO}_4\ (M=98): \frac{9.8\times 10^{-3}}{98}\times 7 = 7\times 10^{-4}\,N_A \to \text{IV}$$

$$\text{C. C}\ (M=12): \frac{1.8\times 10^{-3}}{12}\times 1 = 1.5\times 10^{-4}\,N_A \to \text{II}$$

$$\text{D. NaCl}\ (M=58.5): \frac{5.85\times 10^{-3}}{58.5}\times 2 = 2\times 10^{-4}\,N_A \to \text{I}$$<p>Thus A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> C</p></div></details>
