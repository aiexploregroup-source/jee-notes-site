In order to oxidise a mixture of 1 mole each of $\text{FeC}_2\text{O}_4$, $\text{Fe}_2(\text{C}_2\text{O}_4)_3$, $\text{FeSO}_4$ and $\text{Fe}_2(\text{SO}_4)_3$ in acidic medium, the number of moles of $\text{KMnO}_4$ required is:

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Each $\text{MnO}_4^-$ accepts $5$ electrons in acidic medium. Count the electrons the oxidisable species must give up.</p><p><strong>Per mole of each compound:</strong></p><ul><li>$\text{FeC}_2\text{O}_4$: $\text{Fe}^{2+}\!\to\!\text{Fe}^{3+}$ ($1e^-$) $+\ \text{C}_2\text{O}_4^{2-}\!\to\!2\text{CO}_2$ ($2e^-$) $= 3e^-$</li><li>$\text{Fe}_2(\text{C}_2\text{O}_4)_3$: Fe already $+3$; $3$ oxalates $\to 3\times 2 = 6e^-$</li><li>$\text{FeSO}_4$: $\text{Fe}^{2+}\!\to\!\text{Fe}^{3+} = 1e^-$</li><li>$\text{Fe}_2(\text{SO}_4)_3$: nothing oxidisable $= 0e^-$</li></ul><p><strong>Total electrons released:</strong></p>$$3 + 6 + 1 + 0 = 10\ e^-$$<p><strong>Moles of $\text{KMnO}_4$:</strong></p>$$n = \frac{10}{5} = 2$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B (2)</p></div></details>

An electrochemical cell is constructed using half cells in the direction of spontaneous change
$\mathrm{Fe(OH)}_2(s) + 2e^- \rightarrow \mathrm{Fe}(s) + 2\mathrm{OH}^-(aq)$, $E^0 = -0.88$ V and $\mathrm{AgBr}(s) + e^- \rightarrow \mathrm{Ag}(s) + \mathrm{Br}^-(aq)$, $E^0 = +0.07$ V. Which of the following option is correct?

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>For a spontaneous cell, the half-cell with the <strong>higher</strong> $E^0$ is the cathode (reduction).</p><ul><li>Cathode (higher $E^0$): $\text{AgBr}/\text{Ag}$, $E^0 = +0.07$ V</li><li>Anode (lower $E^0$): $\text{Fe(OH)}_2/\text{Fe}$, $E^0 = -0.88$ V</li></ul>$$E^0_{cell} = E^0_{cath} - E^0_{an} = 0.07 - (-0.88) = +0.95\ \text{V}\ (&gt;0,\ \text{spontaneous})$$<p><strong>Half-reactions:</strong></p>$$\text{Anode (oxidation): } \text{Fe}(s) + 2\text{OH}^- \to \text{Fe(OH)}_2(s) + 2e^-$$<p></p>$$\text{Cathode (reduction): } 2\text{AgBr}(s) + 2e^- \to 2\text{Ag}(s) + 2\text{Br}^-$$<p><strong>Overall reaction:</strong></p>$$\text{Fe}(s) + 2\text{OH}^-(aq) + 2\text{AgBr}(s) \rightleftharpoons \text{Fe(OH)}_2(s) + 2\text{Ag}(s) + 2\text{Br}^-(aq)$$<p>This matches option A. (Fe is oxidised, not reduced; $E^0_{cell} = +0.95$ V, not $-0.95$; $E^0_{cell}$ is intensive.)</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> A</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 4, Shift 2

An electrochemical cell consists of the following two redox couples, $\text{M}^{x+}(aq)/\text{M}(s)$ [$E^\ominus_{red} = +0.15$ V] and $\text{Fe}^{3+}(aq)/\text{Fe}(s)$ [$E^\ominus_{red} = -0.036$ V]. The cell EMF ($E_{cell}$) is recorded to be $0.2057$ V. If the reaction quotient of the electrochemical reaction is found to be $10^{-2}$, then the value of $x$ is __________. (Nearest integer) [Given: M is a p-block metal and $\dfrac{2.303RT}{F} = 0.059$ V]

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>$\text{M}^{x+}/\text{M}$ has the higher $E^\ominus$, so it is the <strong>cathode</strong>; $\text{Fe}^{3+}/\text{Fe}$ is the <strong>anode</strong>.</p>$$E^\ominus_{cell} = 0.15 - (-0.036) = 0.186\ \text{V}$$<p><strong>Nernst equation</strong> with $Q = 10^{-2}$ ($\log_{10}Q = -2$):</p>$$E_{cell} = E^\ominus_{cell} - \frac{0.059}{n}\log_{10}Q$$<p></p>$$0.2057 = 0.186 - \frac{0.059}{n}(-2) = 0.186 + \frac{0.118}{n}$$<p></p>$$\frac{0.118}{n} = 0.0197 \implies n = \frac{0.118}{0.0197} \approx 6$$<p><strong>Balance electrons</strong> in the overall reaction:</p><ul><li>Anode: $\text{Fe} \to \text{Fe}^{3+} + 3e^-$</li><li>Cathode: $\text{M}^{x+} + x\,e^- \to \text{M}$</li></ul><p>Total transferred $n = 3x$ (LCM of the two half-reactions), so:</p>$$3x = 6 \implies x = 2$$<p>(A $+2$ p-block cation, e.g. $\text{Sn}^{2+}$/$\text{Pb}^{2+}$, is consistent.)</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> 2</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 2, Shift 2

Consider the following two half-cell reactions along with the standard reduction potential given:

$$CO_2 + 6H^+ + 6e^- \longrightarrow CH_3OH + H_2O \qquad E^\circ_{red} = 0.02\,\text{V}$$

$$\tfrac{1}{2}O_2 + 2H^+ + 2e^- \longrightarrow H_2O \qquad E^\circ_{red} = 1.23\,\text{V}$$

A fuel cell was set up using the above two reactions such that the cell operates under the standard condition of 1 bar pressure and 298 K temperature. The fuel cell works with 80% efficiency. If the work derived from the cell using 1 mol of $CH_3OH$ is used to compress an ideal gas isothermally against a constant pressure of 1 kPa, then the change in the volume of the gas, $\Delta V =$ __________ m$^3$. (nearest integer) Given: $F = 96500$ C mol$^{-1}$

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>In the fuel cell, $\text{CH}_3\text{OH}$ is the fuel (oxidised at the anode); $\text{O}_2$ is reduced at the cathode.</p>$$E^\circ_{cell} = E^\circ_{cath} - E^\circ_{an} = 1.23 - 0.02 = 1.21\ \text{V}$$<p>Oxidation of $1$ mol $\text{CH}_3\text{OH}\to\text{CO}_2$ transfers $n = 6$ electrons.</p><p><strong>Maximum electrical work:</strong></p>$$W_{max} = nFE^\circ_{cell} = 6 \times 96500 \times 1.21 = 700{,}590\ \text{J}$$<p><strong>Useful work at 80% efficiency:</strong></p>$$W = 0.80 \times 700{,}590 = 560{,}472\ \text{J}$$<p><strong>Isothermal compression</strong> against constant $P = 1\ \text{kPa} = 1000\ \text{Pa}$:</p>$$W = P\,|\Delta V| \implies |\Delta V| = \frac{560{,}472}{1000} = 560.47\ \text{m}^3$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 560</p></div></details>

For a general redox reaction — Anode: $\text{Red}_1\to\text{Ox}_1^{n_1+}+n_1 e^-$; Cathode: $\text{Ox}_2+n_2 e^-\to\text{Red}_2^{n_2-}$ — which of the following statements is **incorrect**?

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Evaluate each statement.</p><ul><li><strong>Option 1 (correct):</strong> Cross-multiplying to balance electrons ($n_1 n_2$ each) gives $n_2\text{Red}_1+n_1\text{Ox}_2 \rightleftharpoons n_2\text{Ox}_1^{n_1+}+n_1\text{Red}_2^{n_2-}$. ✓</li><li><strong>Option 2 (correct):</strong> Electrons produced at the anode are consumed at the cathode, so they cancel in the overall equation. ✓</li><li><strong>Option 3 (correct):</strong> From the Nernst equation $E = E^\circ - \dfrac{RT}{nF}\ln Q$, rearranging gives $\dfrac{E-E^\circ}{RT/F} = -\dfrac{2.303}{n}\log_{10}Q$, so the slope magnitude $\propto \dfrac{1}{n}$. ✓</li><li><strong>Option 4 (INCORRECT):</strong> Electrical work $= \text{charge}\times\text{potential difference}$ (a <strong>product</strong>), i.e. $W = qE$, not the <strong>ratio</strong> $q/E$. ✗</li></ul><p><strong>Answer:</strong> D</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 6, Shift 2

500 mL of 0.2 M $\text{MnO}_4^-$ solution in basic medium when mixed with 500 mL of 1.5 M KI solution, oxidises iodide ions to liberate molecular iodine. This liberated iodine is then titrated with a standard $x$ M thiosulphate solution in presence of starch till the end point. If 300 mL of thiosulphate was consumed, then the value of $x$ is __________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Moles of $\text{MnO}_4^-$:</strong> $0.500 \times 0.2 = 0.1$ mol.</p><p>In <strong>basic (neutral) medium</strong>, $\text{MnO}_4^-$ is reduced to $\text{MnO}_2$, gaining $3\,e^-$ each:</p>$$\text{electrons available} = 0.1 \times 3 = 0.3\ \text{mol}\,e^-$$<p>Iodide is oxidised $2I^- \to I_2 + 2e^-$ ($1\,e^-$ per $I^-$), so $0.3$ mol $I^-$ is oxidised:</p>$$n_{I_2} = \frac{0.3}{2} = 0.15\ \text{mol}$$<p>(KI available $= 0.75$ mol, so $\text{MnO}_4^-$ is limiting — consistent.)</p><p><strong>Thiosulphate titration:</strong> $I_2 + 2\text{S}_2\text{O}_3^{2-} \to 2I^- + \text{S}_4\text{O}_6^{2-}$</p>$$n_{\text{thio}} = 2 \times 0.15 = 0.30\ \text{mol}$$

$$x = \frac{n_{\text{thio}}}{V} = \frac{0.30}{0.300} = 1\ \text{M}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 1</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 5, Shift 2

One half cell in a voltaic cell is constructed by dipping a silver rod in $\text{AgNO}_3$ solution of unknown concentration; the other half cell is a Zn rod dipped in 1 molar $\text{ZnSO}_4$. A voltage of 1.60 V is measured at 298 K. What is the concentration of $\text{Ag}^+$ ions in terms of $\log x$ ($x = [\text{Ag}^+]$)? Given $E^\circ_{Zn^{2+}/Zn} = -0.76$ V, $E^\circ_{Ag^+/Ag} = +0.80$ V, $\dfrac{2.303RT}{F} = 0.059$ V.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Ag is the cathode, Zn the anode:</p>$$E^\circ_{cell} = 0.80 - (-0.76) = 1.56\ \text{V}$$<p>Overall: $\text{Zn} + 2\text{Ag}^+ \to \text{Zn}^{2+} + 2\text{Ag}$, with $n = 2$ and</p>$$Q = \frac{[\text{Zn}^{2+}]}{[\text{Ag}^+]^2} = \frac{1}{x^2}$$<p><strong>Nernst equation:</strong></p>$$E_{cell} = E^\circ_{cell} - \frac{0.059}{2}\log_{10}\frac{1}{x^2} = 1.56 - \frac{0.059}{2}\,(-2\log x) = 1.56 + 0.059\log x$$

$$1.60 = 1.56 + 0.059\log x \implies 0.04 = 0.059\log x$$

$$\log x = \frac{0.04}{0.059} = \frac{4}{5.9}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 5, Shift 2

At 298 K, the molar conductivity of $x\%$ (w/w) MX solution (aqueous) is 123.5 S cm$^2$ mol$^{-1}$. The conductance of the same solution is $1.9 \times 10^{-3}$ S. The value of $x$ is __________ $\times 10^{-2}$. (Given: cell constant $= 1.3$ cm$^{-1}$; molar mass of MX is 75 g mol$^{-1}$; density of aqueous solution of MX at 298 K is 1.0 g mL$^{-1}$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Conductivity</strong> $\kappa = G \times \text{(cell constant)}$:</p>$$\kappa = (1.9\times10^{-3}) \times 1.3 = 2.47\times10^{-3}\ \text{S cm}^{-1}$$<p><strong>Concentration</strong> from $\Lambda_m = \dfrac{1000\,\kappa}{C}$:</p>$$C = \frac{1000\,\kappa}{\Lambda_m} = \frac{1000 \times 2.47\times10^{-3}}{123.5} = 0.02\ \text{mol L}^{-1}$$<p><strong>Mass of MX per litre:</strong> $0.02 \times 75 = 1.5$ g.
Mass of solution per litre (density $1.0$ g mL$^{-1}$) $= 1000$ g.</p><p><strong>Percentage (w/w):</strong></p>$$x\% = \frac{1.5}{1000}\times100 = 0.15\% = 15\times10^{-2}\%$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 15</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 8, Shift 2

Given at 298 K: $E^{\ominus}_{Fe^{2+}/Fe} = X$ Volt and $E^{\ominus}_{Fe^{3+}/Fe} = Y$ Volt. The $E^{\ominus}_{Fe^{3+}/Fe^{2+}}$ in Volt at 298 K is given by:

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Potentials are not additive, but Gibbs energies ($\Delta G^\circ = -nFE^\circ$) are. Combine the half-reactions:</p>$$\text{Fe}^{2+} + 2e^- \to \text{Fe} \quad (n=2):\ \Delta G_1 = -2FX$$<p></p>$$\text{Fe}^{3+} + 3e^- \to \text{Fe} \quad (n=3):\ \Delta G_2 = -3FY$$<p>Target: $\text{Fe}^{3+} + e^- \to \text{Fe}^{2+}\ (n=1)$, obtained as $\Delta G_2 - \Delta G_1$:</p>$$\Delta G_3 = \Delta G_2 - \Delta G_1 = -3FY - (-2FX) = -3FY + 2FX$$<p>Since $\Delta G_3 = -1\cdot F\cdot E^\ominus_{Fe^{3+}/Fe^{2+}}$:</p>$$-F\,E^\ominus = -3FY + 2FX \implies E^\ominus_{Fe^{3+}/Fe^{2+}} = 3Y - 2X$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B</p></div></details>
