Consider the first order reaction $R \to P$. The fraction of molecules decomposed in the given first order reaction can be expressed as

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>For a first order reaction, the integrated rate law is:</p>$$[R] = [R]_0\, e^{-k_1 t}$$<p>Fraction remaining:</p>$$\frac{[R]}{[R]_0} = e^{-k_1 t}$$<p>Fraction decomposed = total minus fraction remaining:</p>$$f = 1 - \frac{[R]}{[R]_0} = 1 - e^{-k_1 t}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> D</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 6 Apr, Shift 1

Sucrose hydrolyses in acidic medium into glucose and fructose by first order rate law with $t_{1/2} = 3$ hour. The percentage of sucrose remaining after 6 hours is ________. (Nearest integer) (Given : $\log 2 = 0.3010$ and $\log 3 = 0.4771$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The reaction is first order, so the fraction remaining after $n$ half-lives is $(1/2)^n$.</p><p>Number of half-lives in 6 hours:</p>$$n = \frac{6}{t_{1/2}} = \frac{6}{3} = 2$$<p>Fraction remaining:</p>$$\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2} = \frac{1}{4} = 0.25$$<p>Percentage remaining:</p>$$0.25 \times 100 = 25\%$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 25</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 1

$t_{100\%}$ is the time required for the 100% completion of the reaction while $t_{1/2}$ is the time required for 50% of the reaction to be completed. Which of the following option correctly represents the relation between $t_{100\%}$ and $t_{1/2}$ for zero and first order reactions respectively?

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Zero order:</strong> $[A] = [A]_0 - kt$. The reaction finishes when $[A] = 0$:</p>$$t_{100\%} = \frac{[A]_0}{k}, \qquad t_{1/2} = \frac{[A]_0}{2k}$$

$$\Rightarrow t_{100\%} = 2\,t_{1/2}$$<p><strong>First order:</strong> $[A] = [A]_0 e^{-kt}$. The concentration approaches zero only as $t \to \infty$, so the reaction is never truly 100% complete in finite time:</p>$$t_{100\%} \to \infty = (t_{1/2})^{\infty}$$<p>Hence zero order gives $t_{100\%} = 2t_{1/2}$ and first order gives $t_{100\%} = (t_{1/2})^{\infty}$.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> B</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 1

For reaction A $\rightarrow$ P, rate constant $k = 1.5 \times 10^3$ s$^{-1}$ at 27°C. If activation energy for the above reaction is 60 kJ mol$^{-1}$, then the temperature (in °C) at which rate constant, $k = 4.5 \times 10^3$ s$^{-1}$ is __________. (Nearest integer)
Given : $\log 2 = 0.30$, $\log 3 = 0.48$, R = 8.3 J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$, $\ln 10 = 2.3$

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Using the two-temperature Arrhenius form:</p>$$\ln\frac{k_2}{k_1} = \frac{E_a}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_1} - \frac{1}{T_2}\right)$$<p>Here $\dfrac{k_2}{k_1} = \dfrac{4.5\times10^3}{1.5\times10^3} = 3$, so:</p>$$\ln 3 = \ln 10 \times \log 3 = 2.3 \times 0.48 = 1.104$$<p>With $T_1 = 300\,\text{K}$, $E_a = 60000\,\text{J mol}^{-1}$, $R = 8.3$:</p>$$\frac{1}{T_2} = \frac{1}{T_1} - \frac{R\,\ln 3}{E_a} = \frac{1}{300} - \frac{8.3 \times 1.104}{60000}$$

$$\frac{1}{T_2} = 3.333\times10^{-3} - 1.527\times10^{-4} = 3.181\times10^{-3}$$

$$T_2 \approx 314.4\,\text{K} = 41.4\,°\text{C}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 41</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 4 Apr, Shift 2

For a first order reaction $A \to B$, given the data: at $t = 0$ min, $[A] = 0.6500$ M; at $t = x$ min, $[A] = 0.0650$ M; at $t = 20$ min, $[A] = 0.00065$ M. $x = $ __________ min. (Nearest integer)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>For a first order reaction:</p>$$k = \frac{1}{t}\ln\frac{[A]_0}{[A]}$$<p>From $t = 0$ to $t = 20$ min, the concentration drops from $0.6500$ to $0.00065$, a factor of $1000$:</p>$$k = \frac{1}{20}\ln(1000) = \frac{\ln 10^3}{20} = \frac{3 \times 2.303}{20} = 0.3454\ \text{min}^{-1}$$<p>For the drop from $0.6500$ to $0.0650$ (a factor of $10$):</p>$$x = \frac{1}{k}\ln(10) = \frac{2.303}{0.3454} \approx 6.67\ \text{min}$$<p>Equivalently, since $0.00065$ is $10^3$ times smaller reached in 20 min, a $10^1$ drop takes one-third of that time: $x = 20/3 \approx 6.67$.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> 7</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 2

Consider the reaction $aX \rightarrow bY$, for which the rate constant at $30\,°C$ is $1 \times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}^{-1}\,\text{L}\,\text{s}^{-1}$. Which of the following statements are true ?
A. When concentration of ‘X’ is increased to four times, the rate of reaction becomes 16 times.
B. The reaction is a second order reaction.
C. The half-life period is independent of the concentration of X.
D. Decomposition of $N_2O_5$ is an example of the above reaction.
E. $\ln\dfrac{[R_0]}{[R]}$ versus time (a straight line through origin) is valid for the above reaction.
Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The units of $k$ are $\text{mol}^{-1}\,\text{L}\,\text{s}^{-1} = \text{L mol}^{-1}\text{s}^{-1}$, which correspond to a <strong>second order</strong> reaction. For an $n$-th order reaction, units of $k$ are $(\text{mol L}^{-1})^{1-n}\text{s}^{-1}$; matching gives $1-n = -1 \Rightarrow n = 2$.</p><ul><li><strong>A:</strong> Rate $= k[X]^2$. If $[X] \to 4[X]$, rate becomes $4^2 = 16$ times. <strong>True.</strong></li><li><strong>B:</strong> Second order, as shown above. <strong>True.</strong></li><li><strong>C:</strong> For second order, $t_{1/2} = \dfrac{1}{k[X]_0}$, which depends on initial concentration. <strong>False.</strong></li><li><strong>D:</strong> Decomposition of $N_2O_5$ is a well-known <strong>first order</strong> reaction. <strong>False.</strong></li><li><strong>E:</strong> $\ln\dfrac{[R_0]}{[R]}$ vs $t$ being linear is characteristic of <strong>first order</strong>, not this second order reaction. <strong>False.</strong></li></ul><p>Only A and B are true.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> A</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 2

Consider the following gas phase reaction being carried out in a closed vessel at $25\,°C$.

$$2A(g) \longrightarrow 4B(g) + C(g)$$

Given data: at time 30 min total pressure of the system = 300 mm Hg; at time $\infty$ total pressure = 600 mm Hg. The pressure of $C(g)$ at 30 minutes time interval would be __________ mm Hg. (nearest integer)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Let the initial pressure of $A$ be $p_0$ (only $A$ present at $t = 0$).</p><p>Stoichiometry: 2 mol $A$ produce $4 + 1 = 5$ mol of gas. When all $A$ is consumed, total pressure becomes:</p>$$P_\infty = \frac{5}{2}p_0 = 600 \Rightarrow p_0 = 240\ \text{mm Hg}$$<p>Let $2\alpha$ be the pressure of $A$ reacted at 30 min. Then:</p>$$A: p_0 - 2\alpha,\quad B: 4\alpha,\quad C: \alpha$$

$$P_{30} = (p_0 - 2\alpha) + 4\alpha + \alpha = p_0 + 3\alpha = 300$$

$$240 + 3\alpha = 300 \Rightarrow \alpha = 20\ \text{mm Hg}$$<p>The pressure of $C$ at 30 min is $\alpha = 20$ mm Hg.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> 20</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 6 Apr, Shift 2

Decomposition of a hydrocarbon follows the equation $k=(5.5\times10^{11}\,\text{s}^{-1})\,e^{\frac{-28000\,K}{T}}$. The activation energy of reaction is __________ kJ mol$^{-1}$. (Nearest Integer) Given: R = 8.3 J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The Arrhenius equation is:</p>$$k = A\, e^{-E_a/RT}$$<p>Comparing the exponent with the given expression:</p>$$\frac{E_a}{RT} = \frac{28000\,\text{K}}{T} \Rightarrow \frac{E_a}{R} = 28000\,\text{K}$$

$$E_a = 28000 \times 8.3 = 232400\ \text{J mol}^{-1} = 232.4\ \text{kJ mol}^{-1}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 232</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 5 Apr, Shift 1

First order gas phase reaction

$$A \to B + C$$

$p_i$ = initial pressure of gas A, $p_t$ = total pressure of the reaction mixture at time $t$. Expression of rate constant ($k$) is

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Let $x$ be the pressure of $A$ that has decomposed at time $t$.</p><table><thead><tr><th>Species</th><th>$A$</th><th>$B$</th><th>$C$</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Pressure at $t$</td><td>$p_i - x$</td><td>$x$</td><td>$x$</td></tr></tbody></table><p>Total pressure:</p>$$p_t = (p_i - x) + x + x = p_i + x \Rightarrow x = p_t - p_i$$<p>Pressure of $A$ remaining:</p>$$p_A = p_i - x = p_i - (p_t - p_i) = 2p_i - p_t$$<p>For a first order reaction:</p>$$k = \frac{1}{t}\ln\frac{p_i}{p_A} = \frac{1}{t}\ln\frac{p_i}{2p_i - p_t}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> A</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 5 Apr, Shift 1

If the half life of a first order reaction is 6.93 minutes then the time required for completion of 99% of the reaction will be ________ minutes. (Given : $\log 2 = 0.3010$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Rate constant from half-life:</p>$$k = \frac{0.693}{t_{1/2}} = \frac{0.693}{6.93} = 0.1\ \text{min}^{-1}$$<p>For 99% completion, $[A]/[A]_0 = 0.01$, i.e. $[A]_0/[A] = 100$:</p>$$t = \frac{2.303}{k}\log\frac{[A]_0}{[A]} = \frac{2.303}{0.1}\log 100 = \frac{2.303}{0.1}\times 2$$

$$t = 23.03 \times 2 = 46.06 \approx 46\ \text{min}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 46</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 5 Apr, Shift 2

For a reaction $A \to P$ at T K, the half life ($t_{1/2}$) is plotted as a function of initial concentration $[A]_0$ of A. The plot is a straight line through the origin. It passes through the point where $[A]_0 = 4 \times 10^{-3}\ \text{mol L}^{-1}$ gives $t_{1/2} = 240$ s, and at $[A]_0 = 1.5 \times 10^{-3}\ \text{mol L}^{-1}$, $t_{1/2} = x$. The value of $x$ is __________ s (Nearest integer)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The plot of $t_{1/2}$ versus $[A]_0$ is a straight line through the origin, so:</p>$$t_{1/2} \propto [A]_0 \Rightarrow \frac{t_{1/2}}{[A]_0} = \text{constant}$$<p>(This is the signature of a <strong>second order</strong> reaction, where $t_{1/2} = \dfrac{1}{k[A]_0}$&amp;mldr; but here the direct proportionality is read straight from the linear plot.)</p><p>Using the given data point:</p>$$\frac{240}{4\times10^{-3}} = \frac{x}{1.5\times10^{-3}}$$

$$x = 240 \times \frac{1.5\times10^{-3}}{4\times10^{-3}} = 240 \times 0.375 = 90\ \text{s}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 90</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 8 Apr, Shift 2

Given below are two statements ($R = 8.314$ J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$ and 1 cal = 4.2 J):

**Statement I :** When $E_a = 12.6$ kcal/mol, the room temperature rate constant is doubled by a 10 $^\circ$C increase in temperature (298 K to 308 K).

**Statement II :** For a first order reaction $A \rightarrow B$, the plot of $t_{1/2}$ versus $[A]_0$ is a straight line through the origin (where $[A]_0$ is the initial concentration and $t_{1/2}$ is the half life).

In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer from the options given below :

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Statement I:</strong> Convert $E_a = 12.6\ \text{kcal mol}^{-1} = 12.6 \times 4.2 \times 10^3 = 52920\ \text{J mol}^{-1}$.</p>$$\ln\frac{k_2}{k_1} = \frac{E_a}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_1} - \frac{1}{T_2}\right) = \frac{52920}{8.314}\left(\frac{1}{298} - \frac{1}{308}\right)$$

$$= 6365.9 \times (1.0895\times10^{-4}) \approx 0.693$$

$$\frac{k_2}{k_1} = e^{0.693} \approx 2$$<p>The rate constant doubles. <strong>Statement I is true.</strong></p><p><strong>Statement II:</strong> For a first order reaction, $t_{1/2} = \dfrac{0.693}{k}$, which is <strong>independent</strong> of $[A]_0$. Its plot against $[A]_0$ is a horizontal line, not a line through the origin. <strong>Statement II is false.</strong></p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> C</p></div></details>
