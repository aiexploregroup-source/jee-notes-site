# JEE Formula Sheets — Chapter Wise

Quick-revision formula sheets for every JEE Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics chapter — every formula with conditions of validity, in one place per chapter.

One page per chapter with **every formula you need for JEE Main and Advanced** — stated precisely, with the conditions under which each applies. Built for the last hour before a test: skim a chapter’s sheet top to bottom and you’ve touched every result that can be asked.

Formula sheets work best as a recall check, not a first exposure: if a formula on the sheet surprises you, go back to that chapter’s full notes (linked from each sheet) and rebuild it from the derivation. For applying them under time pressure, follow up with the chapter’s [previous year questions](../previous-year-questions/index.md).
