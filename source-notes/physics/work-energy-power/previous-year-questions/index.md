PYQ · Apr 4, Shift 1

A 1 kg block subjected to two simultaneous forces $(2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} + 4\hat{k})$ N and $(3\hat{i} - \hat{j} - 2\hat{k})$ N is moved a distance of 25 m along $(3\hat{i} - 4\hat{j})$ direction. The work done in this process is _____ J.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Net force:</strong></p>$$\vec{F} = (2+3)\hat{i} + (3-1)\hat{j} + (4-2)\hat{k} = 5\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}\ \text{N}$$<p><strong>Displacement</strong> of magnitude 25 m along $(3\hat{i} - 4\hat{j})$. Unit vector:</p>$$\hat{d} = \frac{3\hat{i} - 4\hat{j}}{\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2}} = \frac{3\hat{i} - 4\hat{j}}{5}$$<p></p>$$\vec{s} = 25\,\hat{d} = 15\hat{i} - 20\hat{j}\ \text{m}$$<p><strong>Work done:</strong></p>$$W = \vec{F}\cdot\vec{s} = (5)(15) + (2)(-20) + (2)(0) = 75 - 40 = 35\ \text{J}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 35</p></div></details>

PYQ · Apr 4, Shift 1

A body of mass 2 kg begins to move under the influence of time dependent force $\vec{F} = (2t\hat{i} + 6t^2\hat{j})$ N, where $\hat{i}$ and $\hat{j}$ are unit vectors along $x$ and $y$-axis respectively. The power produced by the force at $t = 2$ s is _____ W.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Acceleration</strong> ($m = 2$ kg):</p>$$\vec{a} = \frac{\vec{F}}{m} = t\hat{i} + 3t^2\hat{j}$$<p><strong>Velocity</strong> (body starts from rest, integrate from $0$):</p>$$\vec{v} = \int_0^t \vec{a}\,dt = \frac{t^2}{2}\hat{i} + t^3\hat{j}$$<p>At $t = 2$ s:</p>$$\vec{v} = 2\hat{i} + 8\hat{j}\ \text{m/s}, \qquad \vec{F} = 4\hat{i} + 24\hat{j}\ \text{N}$$<p><strong>Power:</strong></p>$$P = \vec{F}\cdot\vec{v} = (4)(2) + (24)(8) = 8 + 192 = 200\ \text{W}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 200</p></div></details>

PYQ · Apr 6, Shift 1

The rain drop of mass 1 g, starts with zero velocity from a height of 1 km. It hits the ground with a speed of 5 m/s. The work done by the unknown resistive force is ________ J. (take $g = 10$ m/s$^2$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Data: $m = 1\ \text{g} = 10^{-3}\ \text{kg}$, $h = 1\ \text{km} = 1000\ \text{m}$, $v = 5\ \text{m/s}$.</p><p><strong>Work-energy theorem</strong> (gravity + resistive force):</p>$$W_{grav} + W_{res} = \Delta KE = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$$<p><strong>Work by gravity:</strong></p>$$W_{grav} = mgh = (10^{-3})(10)(1000) = 10\ \text{J}$$<p><strong>Change in kinetic energy:</strong></p>$$\Delta KE = \tfrac{1}{2}(10^{-3})(5)^2 = 0.0125\ \text{J}$$<p><strong>Resistive work:</strong></p>$$W_{res} = \Delta KE - W_{grav} = 0.0125 - 10 = -9.9875 \approx -9.98\ \text{J}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> D ($-9.98$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · Apr 2, Shift 2

A spherical ball of mass 2 kg falls from a height of 10 m and is brought to rest after penetrating 10 cm into sand. The average force exerted by sand on the ball is __________ N. (Take $g = 10\,\text{m/s}^2$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Data: $m = 2\ \text{kg}$, $h = 10\ \text{m}$, penetration $d = 10\ \text{cm} = 0.1\ \text{m}$.</p><p>The ball starts and ends at rest, so total kinetic energy change is zero. Over the whole fall-plus-penetration, gravity acts through $(h + d)$ while the average sand force $F$ acts through $d$ opposing motion:</p>$$mg(h + d) - F\,d = 0$$<p><strong>Solve for $F$:</strong></p>$$F = \frac{mg(h + d)}{d} = \frac{(2)(10)(10 + 0.1)}{0.1} = \frac{202}{0.1} = 2020\ \text{N}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B ($2020$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · Apr 6, Shift 2

A body of mass 1 kg moves along a straight line with a velocity $v=2x^2$. The work done by the body during displacement from $x=0$ to $x=5$ m is __________ J.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>By the <strong>work-energy theorem</strong>, work done equals the change in kinetic energy:</p>$$W = \tfrac{1}{2}m\left(v_f^2 - v_i^2\right)$$<p><strong>Velocities</strong> from $v = 2x^2$:</p><ul><li>At $x = 0$: $v_i = 2(0)^2 = 0$</li><li>At $x = 5$: $v_f = 2(5)^2 = 50\ \text{m/s}$</li></ul><p><strong>Work done:</strong></p>$$W = \tfrac{1}{2}(1)\left(50^2 - 0^2\right) = \tfrac{1}{2}(2500) = 1250\ \text{J}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> C ($1250$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · Apr 5, Shift 2

A mass of 1 kg is kept on a inclined plane with $30^\circ$ inclination with respect to horizontal plane and it is at rest initially. Then the whole assembly is moved up with constant velocity of 4 m/s. The work done by the frictional force in time 2 s is __________ J. (Take $g = 10$ m/s$^2$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Data: $m = 1\ \text{kg}$, $\theta = 30^\circ$, $v = 4\ \text{m/s}$ (constant, vertically up), $t = 2\ \text{s}$.</p><p>The block moves with the assembly at constant velocity, so its acceleration is zero and it does not slide on the incline. Static friction must balance the along-incline component of gravity:</p>$$f = mg\sin\theta = (1)(10)\sin 30^\circ = 5\ \text{N}$$<p>directed <strong>up the incline</strong> to prevent the block from sliding down.</p><p><strong>Vertical displacement</strong> in 2 s:</p>$$s = v\,t = 4 \times 2 = 8\ \text{m (upward)}$$<p>The up-slope friction vector makes angle $\theta$ with the horizontal, so its vertical component is $f\sin\theta$. Work done by friction (displacement is purely vertical):</p>$$W_f = (f\sin\theta)\,s = (5)(\sin 30^\circ)(8) = (5)(0.5)(8) = 20\ \text{J}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> A ($20$)</p></div></details>
