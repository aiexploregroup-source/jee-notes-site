PYQ · 2026 6 Apr, Shift 1

The density $\rho$ of a uniform cylinder is determined by measuring its mass $m$, length $l$ and diameter $d$. The measured values of $m$, $l$ and $d$ are $97.42 \pm 0.02$ g, $8.35 \pm 0.05$ mm and $20.20 \pm 0.02$ mm, respectively. Calculated percentage fractional error in $\rho$ is ________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>For a cylinder, $\rho = \dfrac{m}{V} = \dfrac{m}{\frac{\pi}{4} d^2 l} = \dfrac{4m}{\pi d^2 l}$.</p><p>The maximum fractional error adds contributions from each measured quantity:</p>$$\frac{\Delta \rho}{\rho} = \frac{\Delta m}{m} + 2\,\frac{\Delta d}{d} + \frac{\Delta l}{l}$$<p>Substituting the values:</p>$$\frac{\Delta \rho}{\rho} = \frac{0.02}{97.42} + 2\cdot\frac{0.02}{20.20} + \frac{0.05}{8.35}$$

$$= 0.000205 + 0.001980 + 0.005988 = 0.008173$$

$$\frac{\Delta \rho}{\rho} \times 100 = 0.817\% \approx 0.82\%$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B ($0.82\%$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 6 Apr, Shift 1

The potential energy of a particle changes with distance $x$ from a fixed origin as $V = \dfrac{A\sqrt{x}}{x + B}$, where $A$ and $B$ are constant with appropriate dimensions. The dimensions of $AB$ are ________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Since $x$ and $B$ are added, $B$ must have the dimension of length:</p>$$[B] = [L]$$<p>$V$ is a potential energy, so $[V] = [M L^2 T^{-2}]$. From the expression:</p>$$[V] = \frac{[A]\,[L]^{1/2}}{[L]} = [A]\,[L]^{-1/2}$$

$$[A] = [V]\,[L]^{1/2} = [M L^2 T^{-2}][L^{1/2}] = [M L^{5/2} T^{-2}]$$<p>Therefore:</p>$$[AB] = [M L^{5/2} T^{-2}]\,[L] = [M L^{7/2} T^{-2}]$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> D ($[M^1 L^{7/2} T^{-2}]$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 1

The dimensional formula of $\dfrac{1}{2}\epsilon_0 E^2$ ($\epsilon_0$ = permittivity of vacuum and $E$ = electric field) is $\mathrm{M}^a \mathrm{L}^b \mathrm{T}^c$. The value of $2a - b + c =$ __________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The quantity $\dfrac{1}{2}\epsilon_0 E^2$ is the energy density of the electric field, i.e. energy per unit volume:</p>$$\left[\frac{1}{2}\epsilon_0 E^2\right] = \frac{[\text{Energy}]}{[\text{Volume}]} = \frac{[M L^2 T^{-2}]}{[L^3]} = [M^1 L^{-1} T^{-2}]$$<p>So $a = 1$, $b = -1$, $c = -2$. Then:</p>$$2a - b + c = 2(1) - (-1) + (-2) = 2 + 1 - 2 = 1$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B ($1$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 2

Dimensions of universal gravitational constant ($G$) in terms of Planck’s constant ($h$), distance ($L$), mass ($M$) and time ($T$) are __________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The dimensions are $[G] = [M^{-1} L^3 T^{-2}]$ and $[h] = [M L^2 T^{-1}]$.</p><p>Write $G = h^a\, L^b\, M^c\, T^d$ and match powers using $[h] = [M L^2 T^{-1}]$:</p><ul><li>Mass: $-1 = a + c$</li><li>Length: $3 = 2a + b$</li><li>Time: $-2 = -a + d$</li></ul><p>Taking $a = 1$: $c = -2$, $b = 3 - 2 = 1$, and $d = -2 + 1 = -1$.</p>$$[G] = [h^1\, T^{-1}\, L^1\, M^{-2}] = [hT^{-1}LM^{-2}]$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B ($[hT^{-1}LM^{-2}]$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 6 Apr, Shift 2

The percentage error in the calculated volume of a sphere, if there is 2% error in its diameter measurement, is __________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The volume of a sphere in terms of diameter $d$:</p>$$V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi \left(\frac{d}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{\pi}{6} d^3 \quad\Rightarrow\quad V \propto d^3$$<p>The fractional error propagates with the power:</p>$$\frac{\Delta V}{V} = 3\,\frac{\Delta d}{d}$$

$$\frac{\Delta V}{V}\times 100 = 3 \times 2\% = 6\%$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> C ($6$)</p></div></details>

Match List - I with List - II.

**List - I:** A. Boltzmann constant; B. Stefan’s constant; C. Planck’s constant; D. Gravitational constant.

**List - II:** I. $[M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}]$; II. $[ML^2T^{-1}]$; III. $[ML^2T^{-2}K^{-1}]$; IV. $[ML^0T^{-3}K^{-4}]$.

Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Determine each dimensional formula:</p><p><strong>A. Boltzmann constant</strong> — from $E = k_B T$: $[k_B] = \dfrac{[M L^2 T^{-2}]}{[K]} = [M L^2 T^{-2} K^{-1}]$ → <strong>III</strong></p><p><strong>B. Stefan’s constant</strong> — from $\dfrac{P}{A} = \sigma T^4$: $[\sigma] = \dfrac{[M T^{-3}]}{[K^4]} = [M L^0 T^{-3} K^{-4}]$ → <strong>IV</strong></p><p><strong>C. Planck’s constant</strong> — from $E = h\nu$: $[h] = \dfrac{[M L^2 T^{-2}]}{[T^{-1}]} = [M L^2 T^{-1}]$ → <strong>II</strong></p><p><strong>D. Gravitational constant</strong> — from $F = \dfrac{G m_1 m_2}{r^2}$: $[G] = \dfrac{[M L T^{-2}][L^2]}{[M^2]} = [M^{-1} L^3 T^{-2}]$ → <strong>I</strong></p><p>So the mapping is A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> C (A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 5 Apr, Shift 1

$L$, $C$ and $R$ represents physical quantities inductance, capacitance and resistance respectively. The dimensional formula $\mathrm{M\,L^2\,T^{-4}\,A^{-2}}$ corresponds to __________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Base dimensions of the components:</p>$$[L] = [M L^2 T^{-2} A^{-2}], \quad [C] = [M^{-1} L^{-2} T^{4} A^{2}], \quad [R] = [M L^2 T^{-3} A^{-2}]$$<p>Test option A, $\dfrac{R}{\sqrt{LC}}$. First $LC$:</p>$$[LC] = [M L^2 T^{-2} A^{-2}][M^{-1} L^{-2} T^{4} A^{2}] = [T^{2}]$$

$$[\sqrt{LC}] = [T]$$<p>Then:</p>$$\left[\frac{R}{\sqrt{LC}}\right] = \frac{[M L^2 T^{-3} A^{-2}]}{[T]} = [M L^2 T^{-4} A^{-2}]$$<p>This matches the target formula exactly.</p><p>(Note: $\sqrt{LC}$ is the time constant of an $LC$ oscillator, and $R/\sqrt{LC}$ therefore carries resistance-per-time dimensions.)</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> A ($\frac{R}{\sqrt{LC}}$)</p></div></details>

Match List - I with List - II.

**List - I:** A. Meter (L); B. Second (S); C. Kilogram (M); D. Kelvin (K)

**List - II:** I. $\sqrt{\dfrac{hc}{G}}$; II. $\sqrt{\dfrac{Gh}{c^5}}$; III. $\sqrt{\dfrac{K^2 L^2 c^3}{Gh}}$; IV. $\sqrt{\dfrac{Gh}{c^3}}$

where $h$ (Planck’s constant), $G$ (gravitational constant) and $c$ (speed of light in vacuum) are fundamental units. Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Use $[h] = [M L^2 T^{-1}]$, $[c] = [L T^{-1}]$, $[G] = [M^{-1} L^3 T^{-2}]$.</p><p><strong>Planck length (A, Meter):</strong> $\sqrt{\dfrac{Gh}{c^3}}$</p>$$\left[\frac{Gh}{c^3}\right] = \frac{[M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}][ML^2T^{-1}]}{[L^3 T^{-3}]} = [L^2] \Rightarrow \sqrt{\cdot} = [L]$$<p>→ <strong>IV</strong></p><p><strong>Planck time (B, Second):</strong> $\sqrt{\dfrac{Gh}{c^5}}$</p>$$\left[\frac{Gh}{c^5}\right] = \frac{[L^5 T^{-3}]}{[L^5 T^{-5}]} = [T^2] \Rightarrow \sqrt{\cdot} = [T]$$<p>→ <strong>II</strong></p><p><strong>Planck mass (C, Kilogram):</strong> $\sqrt{\dfrac{hc}{G}}$</p>$$\left[\frac{hc}{G}\right] = \frac{[ML^2T^{-1}][LT^{-1}]}{[M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}]} = [M^2] \Rightarrow \sqrt{\cdot} = [M]$$<p>→ <strong>I</strong></p><p><strong>Kelvin (D):</strong> the only expression containing $K$ is III → <strong>III</strong></p><p>Mapping: A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> B (A-IV, B-II, C-I, D-III)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 8 Apr, Shift 2

A new unit ($\alpha$) of length is chosen such that it is equal to the speed of light in vacuum. What is the distance between Venus and Earth in terms of $\alpha$ units if light takes 6 min. 40 s to cover this distance?

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The unit of length is defined as $\alpha = c$ (numerically equal to the speed of light).</p><p>Total time taken by light:</p>$$t = 6\ \text{min}\ 40\ \text{s} = 6\times 60 + 40 = 400\ \text{s}$$<p>Distance travelled by light:</p>$$d = c \times t = c \times 400\ \text{s}$$<p>Expressing this in units of $\alpha = c$:</p>$$d = \frac{c \times 400}{c}\,\alpha = 400\,\alpha$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B ($400\,\alpha$)</p></div></details>

Consider the equation $H = \dfrac{x^p\, \epsilon^q\, E^r}{t^s}$, where $H$ = magnetic field; $E$ = electric field, $\epsilon$ = permittivity, $x$ = distance, $t$ = time. The values of $p$, $q$, $r$ and $s$ respectively are:

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Use base dimensions with current $A$:</p>$$[H] = [L^{-1} A]\ (\text{A/m}), \quad [x] = [L], \quad [\epsilon] = [M^{-1}L^{-3}T^{4}A^{2}], \quad [E] = [MLT^{-3}A^{-1}], \quad [t] = [T]$$<p>Test option A, $(p,q,r,s) = (1,1,1,1)$, i.e. $\dfrac{x\,\epsilon\,E}{t}$:</p>$$[x][\epsilon][E] = [L]\,[M^{-1}L^{-3}T^{4}A^{2}]\,[MLT^{-3}A^{-1}]$$<p>Combine: $M$: $-1+1=0$; $L$: $1-3+1=-1$; $T$: $4-3=1$; $A$: $2-1=1$, giving $[L^{-1}T^{1}A^{1}]$.</p><p>Divide by $t$:</p>$$\frac{[L^{-1}T^{1}A^{1}]}{[T]} = [L^{-1}A]$$<p>This equals $[H]$, the dimension of the magnetic field strength ($\text{A/m}$).</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> A ($1, 1, 1, 1$)</p></div></details>
