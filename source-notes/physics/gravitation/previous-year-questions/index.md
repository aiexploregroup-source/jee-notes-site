PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 1

A planet $(P_1)$ is moving around the star of mass $2M$ in the orbit of radius $R$. Another planet $(P_2)$ is moving around another star of mass $4M$ in a orbit of radius $2R$. Ratio of time periods of revolution of $P_2$ and $P_1$ is __________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>For a planet in a circular orbit, the gravitational force provides the centripetal force:</p>$$\frac{GMm}{r^2} = \frac{mr\,\omega^2}{1} = mr\left(\frac{2\pi}{T}\right)^2$$<p>Solving for the time period:</p>$$T^2 = \frac{4\pi^2 r^3}{GM} \quad\Rightarrow\quad T \propto \sqrt{\frac{r^3}{M}}$$<p>Taking the ratio of $P_2$ to $P_1$:</p>$$\frac{T_2}{T_1} = \sqrt{\frac{r_2^{\,3}/M_2}{r_1^{\,3}/M_1}} = \sqrt{\frac{(2R)^3/(4M)}{R^3/(2M)}}$$

$$\frac{T_2}{T_1} = \sqrt{\frac{8R^3/(4M)}{R^3/(2M)}} = \sqrt{\frac{2R^3/M}{0.5\,R^3/M}} = \sqrt{4} = 2$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B ($2$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 4 Apr, Shift 2

The height in terms of radius of the earth ($R$), at which the acceleration due to gravity becomes $\dfrac{g}{9}$, where $g$ is acceleration due to gravity on earth’s surface, is __________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The acceleration due to gravity at height $h$ above the surface is:</p>$$g_h = \frac{g}{\left(1 + \dfrac{h}{R}\right)^2}$$<p>Setting $g_h = \dfrac{g}{9}$:</p>$$\frac{g}{\left(1 + \dfrac{h}{R}\right)^2} = \frac{g}{9} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \left(1 + \frac{h}{R}\right)^2 = 9$$<p>Taking the positive root:</p>$$1 + \frac{h}{R} = 3 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{h}{R} = 2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad h = 2R$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> C ($2R$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 2

If a body of mass 1 kg falls on the earth from infinity, it attains velocity ($v$) and kinetic energy ($k$) on reaching the surface of earth. The values of $v$ and $k$ respectively are __________. (Take radius of earth to be 6400 km and $g = 9.8\,\text{m/s}^2$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>A body falling from infinity reaches the surface with the escape speed (energy conservation gives the same magnitude):</p>$$v = \sqrt{2gR}$$<p>Substituting $g = 9.8\,\text{m/s}^2$ and $R = 6400\,\text{km} = 6.4 \times 10^6\,\text{m}$:</p>$$v = \sqrt{2 \times 9.8 \times 6.4 \times 10^6} = \sqrt{1.2544 \times 10^8}$$

$$v = 1.12 \times 10^4\,\text{m/s} = 11.2\,\text{km/s}$$<p>The kinetic energy gained by the $m = 1\,\text{kg}$ body:</p>$$k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{1}{2}(1)(1.2544 \times 10^8) = 6.27 \times 10^7\,\text{J}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> A ($11.2\,\text{km/s};\ 6.27 \times 10^7\,\text{J}$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 5 Apr, Shift 1

When one moves from a point 16 km below the earth’s surface to a point 16 km above the earth’s surface. The change in $g$ is approximately $\alpha$ %. The value of $\alpha$ is ______. (Take radius of the earth = 6400 km.)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>At a depth $d$ below the surface:</p>$$g_d = g\left(1 - \frac{d}{R}\right)$$<p>At a height $h$ above the surface (small $h$, first-order approximation):</p>$$g_h \approx g\left(1 - \frac{2h}{R}\right)$$<p>The value of $g$ is larger at depth than at height, so the change on moving from the deep point up to the high point is:</p>$$\Delta g = g_d - g_h = g\left[\left(1 - \frac{d}{R}\right) - \left(1 - \frac{2h}{R}\right)\right] = g\left(\frac{2h - d}{R}\right)$$<p>With $d = h = 16\,\text{km}$ and $R = 6400\,\text{km}$:</p>$$\Delta g = g\left(\frac{2(16) - 16}{6400}\right) = g\left(\frac{16}{6400}\right)$$<p>Expressing as a percentage of $g$:</p>$$\alpha = \frac{16}{6400} \times 100 = 0.25\%$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B ($0.25$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 5 Apr, Shift 2

A body of mass $m$ is taken from the surface of earth to a height equal to twice the radius of earth ($R_e$). The increase in potential energy will be __________. ($g$ is acceleration due to gravity at the surface of earth)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The gravitational potential energy at a distance $r$ from the earth’s centre is:</p>$$U = -\frac{GM m}{r}$$<p>At the surface ($r = R_e$) and at height $h = 2R_e$ (so $r = R_e + 2R_e = 3R_e$):</p>$$\Delta U = U_{\text{final}} - U_{\text{initial}} = -\frac{GMm}{3R_e} - \left(-\frac{GMm}{R_e}\right)$$

$$\Delta U = \frac{GMm}{R_e}\left(1 - \frac{1}{3}\right) = \frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{GMm}{R_e}$$<p>Using $g = \dfrac{GM}{R_e^2}$, i.e. $\dfrac{GM}{R_e} = gR_e$:</p>$$\Delta U = \frac{2}{3}\,m g R_e$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> D ($\dfrac{2}{3}mgR_e$)</p></div></details>
