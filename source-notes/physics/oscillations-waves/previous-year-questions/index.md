PYQ · 2026 4 Apr, Shift 1

The velocity of a particle executing simple harmonic motion along $x$-axis is described as $v^2 = 50 - x^2$, where $x$ represents displacement. If the time period of motion is $\frac{x}{7}$ s, the value of $x$ is _____.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>For SHM the velocity satisfies $v^2 = \omega^2\left(A^2 - x^2\right)$.</p><p>Comparing with the given $v^2 = 50 - x^2$:</p>$$\omega^2 = 1 \implies \omega = 1\ \text{rad/s}$$<p>Time period:</p>$$T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega} = 2\pi\ \text{s}$$<p>Given $T = \dfrac{x}{7}$:</p>$$\frac{x}{7} = 2\pi \implies x = 14\pi \approx 43.98 \approx 44$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 44</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 6 Apr, Shift 1

A particle is executing simple harmonic motion. Its amplitude is $A$ and time period is 5 sec. The time required by it to move from $x = A$ to $x = \dfrac{A}{\sqrt{2}}$ is ________ sec.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Start the motion from the extreme position, so use $x = A\cos(\omega t)$ with $x = A$ at $t = 0$.</p>$$\omega = \frac{2\pi}{T} = \frac{2\pi}{5}\ \text{rad/s}$$<p>At $x = \dfrac{A}{\sqrt{2}}$:</p>$$A\cos(\omega t) = \frac{A}{\sqrt{2}} \implies \cos(\omega t) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \implies \omega t = \frac{\pi}{4}$$<p>Therefore:</p>$$t = \frac{\pi/4}{\omega} = \frac{\pi/4}{2\pi/5} = \frac{5}{8}\ \text{s}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> $\dfrac{5}{8}$</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 1

The equation of a plane progressive wave is given by $y = 5\cos\pi\left(200t - \dfrac{x}{150}\right)$ where $x$ and $y$ are in cm and $t$ is in second. The velocity of the wave is __________ m/s.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Write the equation as $y = 5\cos(\omega t - kx)$ with:</p>$$\omega = 200\pi\ \text{rad/s}, \qquad k = \frac{\pi}{150}\ \text{cm}^{-1}$$<p>Wave velocity:</p>$$v = \frac{\omega}{k} = \frac{200\pi}{\pi/150} = 200 \times 150 = 30000\ \text{cm/s}$$<p>Converting to SI units:</p>$$v = 30000\ \text{cm/s} = 300\ \text{m/s}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 300</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 2

The equation of motion of a particle is given by $x = a\sin\left(50t + \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)$ cm. The particle will come to rest at time $t_1$ and it will have zero acceleration at time $t_2$. The $t_1$ and $t_2$ respectively are __________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Here $\omega = 50$ rad/s and phase $\phi = 50t + \dfrac{\pi}{3}$.</p><p><strong>Particle at rest</strong> ($v = 0$): velocity $v = 50a\cos\phi$, so the first instant of rest is at the extreme, $\cos\phi = 0$:</p>$$50t_1 + \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{\pi}{2} \implies 50t_1 = \frac{\pi}{6} \implies t_1 = \frac{\pi}{300}\ \text{s}$$<p><strong>Zero acceleration</strong> ($a = 0$): acceleration $\propto -\sin\phi$, so it vanishes at the mean position, $\sin\phi = 0$:</p>$$50t_2 + \frac{\pi}{3} = \pi \implies 50t_2 = \frac{2\pi}{3} \implies t_2 = \frac{2\pi}{150} = \frac{\pi}{75}\ \text{s}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> $\dfrac{\pi}{300}\,\text{s},\ \dfrac{\pi}{75}\,\text{s}$</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 6 Apr, Shift 2

A spring stretches by 2 mm when it is loaded with a mass of 200 g. From equilibrium position the mass is further pulled down by 2 mm and released. The frequency associated with the system and maximum energy in the spring are __________ Hz and __________ J, respectively. (Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Spring constant</strong> from the static stretch $x_0 = 2\ \text{mm} = 2\times10^{-3}$ m, $m = 0.2$ kg:</p>$$k = \frac{mg}{x_0} = \frac{0.2 \times 10}{2\times10^{-3}} = 1000\ \text{N/m}$$<p><strong>Frequency:</strong></p>$$f = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{1000}{0.2}} = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{5000} = \frac{50\sqrt{2}}{2\pi} = \frac{25\sqrt{2}}{\pi} = \frac{5\sqrt{50}}{\pi}\ \text{Hz}$$<p><strong>Maximum energy in the spring</strong> occurs at maximum extension, which is the static stretch plus the amplitude:</p>$$x_{\max} = 2\ \text{mm} + 2\ \text{mm} = 4\ \text{mm} = 4\times10^{-3}\ \text{m}$$

$$E_{\max} = \frac{1}{2}k\,x_{\max}^2 = \frac{1}{2}(1000)\left(4\times10^{-3}\right)^2 = 500 \times 16\times10^{-6} = 8\times10^{-3}\ \text{J}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> $\dfrac{5\sqrt{50}}{\pi}$ and $8\times10^{-3}$</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 5 Apr, Shift 1

A transverse wave on a string is described by $y = 3\sin(36t + 0.018x + \pi/4)$, where $x, y$ are in cm and $t$ in seconds. The least distance between the two successive crests in the wave is ______ cm. (Nearest integer) ($\pi = 3.14$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The distance between two successive crests equals one wavelength.</p><p>From the wave equation, the wave number is:</p>$$k = 0.018\ \text{cm}^{-1}$$<p>Wavelength:</p>$$\lambda = \frac{2\pi}{k} = \frac{2 \times 3.14}{0.018} = \frac{6.28}{0.018} = 348.9\ \text{cm}$$<p>Nearest integer:</p>$$\lambda \approx 349\ \text{cm}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 349</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 5 Apr, Shift 2

Match List - I with List - II.

**List - I:**
A. $\sin^2 \omega t$
B. $\sin^3(2\omega t)$
C. $\sin(\omega t) + \cos(\pi \omega t)$
D. $\cos \omega t + \cos 2\omega t$

**List - II:**
I. Periodic with time period $T = \dfrac{\pi}{\omega}$ but not simple harmonic motion (SHM)
II. Periodic with time period $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$ but Not SHM
III. Periodic with time period $T = \dfrac{\pi}{\omega}$ and SHM
IV. Non-periodic

Choose the correct answer from the options given below :

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>A. $\sin^2\omega t$:</strong> Using $\sin^2\omega t = \dfrac{1 - \cos 2\omega t}{2}$, this is a single cosine of angular frequency $2\omega$, so period $T = \dfrac{2\pi}{2\omega} = \dfrac{\pi}{\omega}$ and it <strong>is SHM</strong> (about the mean value $\tfrac12$). $\to$ <strong>III</strong></p><p><strong>B. $\sin^3(2\omega t)$:</strong> Using $\sin^3\theta = \dfrac{3\sin\theta - \sin 3\theta}{4}$ with $\theta = 2\omega t$, the lowest frequency term has angular frequency $2\omega$, giving period $\dfrac{2\pi}{2\omega} = \dfrac{\pi}{\omega}$. It is a mix of two frequencies, so <strong>not SHM</strong>. $\to$ <strong>I</strong></p><p><strong>C. $\sin(\omega t) + \cos(\pi\omega t)$:</strong> Individual periods are $\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$ and $\dfrac{2\pi}{\pi\omega} = \dfrac{2}{\omega}$. Their ratio is $\dfrac{2\pi/\omega}{2/\omega} = \pi$, an irrational number, so no common period exists $\Rightarrow$ <strong>non-periodic</strong>. $\to$ <strong>IV</strong></p><p><strong>D. $\cos\omega t + \cos 2\omega t$:</strong> Superposition of two commensurate frequencies with fundamental period $\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}$; a sum of distinct frequencies is <strong>not SHM</strong>. $\to$ <strong>II</strong></p><p>Thus A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> A-III, B-I, C-IV, D-II</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 8 Apr, Shift 2

The frequency of oscillation of a mass $m$ suspended by a spring is $v_1$. If the length of the spring is cut to half, the same mass oscillates with frequency $v_2$. The value of $v_2/v_1$ is __________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The spring constant is inversely proportional to the natural length: $k \propto \dfrac{1}{L}$.</p><p>Cutting the spring to half its length doubles the spring constant:</p>$$k_2 = 2k_1$$<p>Frequency of a spring-mass system:</p>$$v = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} \implies v \propto \sqrt{k}$$<p>Therefore:</p>$$\frac{v_2}{v_1} = \sqrt{\frac{k_2}{k_1}} = \sqrt{2}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> $\sqrt{2}$</p></div></details>
