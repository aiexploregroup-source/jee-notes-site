PYQ · 2026 4 Apr, Shift 1

A parallel plate air capacitor is connected to a battery. The plates are pulled apart at uniform speed $v$. If $x$ is the separation between the plates at any instant, then the time rate of change of electrostatic energy of the capacitor is proportional to $x^\alpha$, where $\alpha$ is _____.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The capacitor stays connected to the battery, so the voltage $V$ is <strong>constant</strong>.</p><p>Capacitance at separation $x$:</p>$$C = \frac{\epsilon_0 A}{x}$$<p>Energy stored:</p>$$U = \frac{1}{2}CV^2 = \frac{\epsilon_0 A V^2}{2x}$$<p>Differentiate with respect to time (with $\dfrac{dx}{dt} = v$):</p>$$\frac{dU}{dt} = \frac{\epsilon_0 A V^2}{2}\cdot\frac{d}{dt}\!\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) = -\frac{\epsilon_0 A V^2}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{x^2}\,v$$

$$\frac{dU}{dt} \propto x^{-2}$$<p>Hence $\alpha = -2$.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> A ($-2$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 6 Apr, Shift 1

A thin half ring of radius 35 cm is uniformly charged with a total charge of $Q$ coulomb. If the magnitude of the electric field at centre of the half ring is 100 V/m, then the value of $Q$ is ________ nC. ($\epsilon_0 = 8.85 \times 10^{-12}$ C$^2$/Nm$^2$ and $\pi = 3.14$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>For a uniformly charged semicircular ring, the field at the centre is directed along the axis of symmetry. With linear charge density $\lambda = \dfrac{Q}{\pi R}$:</p>$$E = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\cdot\frac{2\lambda}{R} = \frac{2\lambda}{4\pi\epsilon_0 R}$$<p>Substitute $\lambda = \dfrac{Q}{\pi R}$:</p>$$E = \frac{2Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0\,\pi R^2} = \frac{Q}{2\pi^2\epsilon_0 R^2}$$<p>Solve for $Q$:</p>$$Q = 2\pi^2\epsilon_0 R^2 E = 2(3.14)^2(8.85\times10^{-12})(0.35)^2(100)$$

$$Q \approx 2.14\times10^{-9}\ \text{C} = 2.14\ \text{nC}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> A (2.14)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 6 Apr, Shift 1

A three coulomb charge moves from the point $(0, -2, -5)$ to the point $(5, 1, 2)$ in an electric field expressed as $\vec{E} = 2x\hat{i} + 3y^2\hat{j} + 4\hat{k}$ N/C. The work done in moving the charge is ________ J.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Work done by the electric force:</p>$$W = q\int \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{l} = q\left[\int_{0}^{5} 2x\,dx + \int_{-2}^{1} 3y^2\,dy + \int_{-5}^{2} 4\,dz\right]$$<p>Evaluate each integral:</p>$$\int_{0}^{5} 2x\,dx = \big[x^2\big]_0^5 = 25$$

$$\int_{-2}^{1} 3y^2\,dy = \big[y^3\big]_{-2}^{1} = 1-(-8) = 9$$

$$\int_{-5}^{2} 4\,dz = 4\big[z\big]_{-5}^{2} = 4(7) = 28$$<p>Sum $= 25 + 9 + 28 = 62$. With $q = 3$ C:</p>$$W = 3\times 62 = 186\ \text{J}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 186</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 1

Two charged conducting spheres $S_1$ and $S_2$ of radii 8 cm and 18 cm are connected to each other by a wire. After equilibrium is established, the ratio of electric fields on $S_1$ and $S_2$ spheres are $E_{S_1}$ and $E_{S_2}$ respectively. The value of $\dfrac{E_{S_1}}{E_{S_2}}$ is __________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>When connected by a wire, both spheres reach the <strong>same potential</strong>:</p>$$V = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{Q_1}{r_1} = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{Q_2}{r_2}$$<p>The surface field of a sphere is $E = \dfrac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\dfrac{Q}{r^2} = \dfrac{V}{r}$.</p><p>Since $V$ is common:</p>$$\frac{E_{S_1}}{E_{S_2}} = \frac{V/r_1}{V/r_2} = \frac{r_2}{r_1} = \frac{18}{8} = \frac{9}{4}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> D ($\tfrac{9}{4}$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 4 Apr, Shift 2

A rigid dipole undergoes a simple harmonic motion about its centre in the presence of an electric field $\vec{E}_1 = E_0\,\hat{x}$. If another electric field $\vec{E}_2 = 2E_0(\hat{y} + \hat{z})$ is introduced to the system, what will be the percentage change in the frequency of the oscillation (approximate)?

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>A dipole of moment $p$ and moment of inertia $I$ oscillating in a field $E$ has angular frequency:</p>$$\omega = \sqrt{\frac{pE}{I}} \implies f \propto \sqrt{E}$$<p>Initial field magnitude: $E_1 = E_0$.</p><p>Net field after adding $\vec{E}_2$:</p>$$\vec{E}_{net} = E_0\hat{x} + 2E_0\hat{y} + 2E_0\hat{z}$$

$$|\vec{E}_{net}| = E_0\sqrt{1^2 + 2^2 + 2^2} = 3E_0$$<p>Frequency ratio:</p>$$\frac{f_2}{f_1} = \sqrt{\frac{3E_0}{E_0}} = \sqrt{3} \approx 1.732$$<p>Percentage change $= (\sqrt{3}-1)\times100 \approx 73\%$.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> A (73%)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 4 Apr, Shift 2

**Assertion A:** In electrostatics, a conductor does not store any net charge inside.

**Reason R:** Inside the capacitor (with no dielectric medium), the free charge carriers, if placed between the plates of capacitor, experience force and drift.

Choose the correct answer from the options given below.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Assertion A</strong> is <strong>true</strong>: in electrostatic equilibrium the field inside a conductor is zero, so any net charge resides entirely on the surface — the interior holds no net charge.</p><p><strong>Reason R</strong> is also <strong>true</strong>: a uniform electric field exists in the gap between capacitor plates, so a free charge placed there does experience a force and drifts.</p><p>However, R describes the behaviour of charges in the <strong>vacuum gap between plates</strong>, not the mechanism by which a <strong>conductor’s interior</strong> carries no net charge (which follows from $\vec{E}=0$ inside the conductor). So R does <strong>not</strong> correctly explain A.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> B (Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 2

Two metal plates ($A$, $B$) are kept horizontally with separation of $\left(\dfrac{12}{\pi}\right)$ cm, with plate $A$ on the top. An atomizer jet sprays oil (density $1.5\,\text{g/cm}^3$) droplets of radius 1 mm horizontally. All oil droplets carry a charge 5 nC. The potentials $V_A$ and $V_B$ are required on plates $A$ and $B$ respectively in order to ensure the droplets do not descend. The values of $V_A$ and $V_B$ are __________. (Neglect the air resistance to the droplets and take $g = 10\,\text{m/s}^2$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>For the droplet to stay suspended, the electric force must balance gravity: $qE = mg$.</p><p><strong>Mass of droplet</strong> ($\rho = 1500\ \text{kg/m}^3$, $r = 10^{-3}$ m):</p>$$m = \rho\cdot\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = 1500\cdot\frac{4}{3}\pi(10^{-3})^3 = 2\pi\times10^{-6}\ \text{kg}$$

$$mg = 2\pi\times10^{-6}\times10 = 2\pi\times10^{-5}\ \text{N}$$<p><strong>Required field</strong> ($q = 5\times10^{-9}$ C):</p>$$E = \frac{mg}{q} = \frac{2\pi\times10^{-5}}{5\times10^{-9}} = 4\pi\times10^{3}\ \text{V/m}$$<p><strong>Potential difference</strong> ($d = \tfrac{12}{\pi}\times10^{-2}$ m):</p>$$\Delta V = E\,d = 4\pi\times10^{3}\times\frac{12}{\pi}\times10^{-2} = 480\ \text{V}$$<p>The charge is positive, so the electric force must point <strong>up</strong>, meaning $\vec{E}$ points up (from bottom plate $B$ toward top plate $A$). Thus the <strong>bottom plate $B$ is at higher potential</strong>: $V_B - V_A = 480$ V. Only $V_A = 100$ V, $V_B = 580$ V satisfies this.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> A ($100\,V$ and $580\,V$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 2

Two point charges $8\,\mu C$ and $-2\,\mu C$ are located at $x = 2$ cm and $x = 4$ cm, respectively on the $x$-axis. The ratio of electric flux due to these charges through two spheres of radii 3 cm and 5 cm with their centers at the origin is __________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>By Gauss’s law, flux depends only on the <strong>enclosed charge</strong>:</p>$$\Phi = \frac{Q_{enc}}{\epsilon_0}$$<p><strong>Sphere of radius 3 cm:</strong> encloses the charge at $x = 2$ cm (inside), but not the one at $x = 4$ cm (outside).</p>$$Q_{enc,1} = 8\,\mu C$$<p><strong>Sphere of radius 5 cm:</strong> encloses both charges ($x = 2$ cm and $x = 4$ cm).</p>$$Q_{enc,2} = 8 + (-2) = 6\,\mu C$$<p>Ratio of fluxes:</p>$$\frac{\Phi_1}{\Phi_2} = \frac{8}{6} = \frac{4}{3}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> C ($4 : 3$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 6 Apr, Shift 2

The electric potential as a function of $x,y$ is given by $V=5(x^2-y^2)$ V. The electric field at a point $(2,3)$ m is __________ V/m.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The electric field is the negative gradient of potential:</p>$$\vec{E} = -\nabla V = -\frac{\partial V}{\partial x}\hat{i} - \frac{\partial V}{\partial y}\hat{j}$$<p>With $V = 5(x^2 - y^2)$:</p>$$\frac{\partial V}{\partial x} = 10x, \qquad \frac{\partial V}{\partial y} = -10y$$<p>So:</p>$$\vec{E} = -10x\,\hat{i} + 10y\,\hat{j}$$<p>At $(2, 3)$:</p>$$\vec{E} = -10(2)\hat{i} + 10(3)\hat{j} = -20\hat{i} + 30\hat{j}\ \text{V/m}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> A ($-20\hat{i}+30\hat{j}$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 6 Apr, Shift 2

A sphere of capacitance 100 pF is charged to a potential of 100 V. Another identical uncharged metal sphere is brought in contact with the charged sphere, then the change in the total energy stored on these spheres, when they touch is $\alpha\times10^{-7}$ J. The value of $\alpha$ is __________. (combined capacitance of spheres is 200 pF)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Initial energy</strong> ($C = 100$ pF, $V = 100$ V):</p>$$U_i = \frac{1}{2}CV^2 = \frac{1}{2}(100\times10^{-12})(100)^2 = 5\times10^{-7}\ \text{J}$$<p>Charge is conserved: $Q = CV = 10^{-8}$ C. On contact the combined capacitance is $200$ pF, so the common potential becomes:</p>$$V' = \frac{Q}{C'} = \frac{10^{-8}}{200\times10^{-12}} = 50\ \text{V}$$<p><strong>Final energy:</strong></p>$$U_f = \frac{1}{2}C'V'^2 = \frac{1}{2}(200\times10^{-12})(50)^2 = 2.5\times10^{-7}\ \text{J}$$<p><strong>Change in energy:</strong></p>$$|\Delta U| = U_i - U_f = (5 - 2.5)\times10^{-7} = 2.5\times10^{-7}\ \text{J}$$<p>So $\alpha = \dfrac{5}{2}$ (energy decreases by this amount).</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> B ($\tfrac{5}{2}$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 8 Apr, Shift 2

Two point charges $q_1 = 3\,\mu C$ and $q_2 = -4\,\mu C$ are placed at points $(2\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} + 3\hat{k})$ and $(\hat{i} + \hat{j} + \hat{k})$ respectively. Force on charge $q_2$ is __________ N. $\left(\text{Take } \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} = 9 \times 10^9 \text{ SI Units}\right)$

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Displacement from $q_1$ to $q_2$:</p>$$\vec{r} = (1-2)\hat{i} + (1-3)\hat{j} + (1-3)\hat{k} = -\hat{i} - 2\hat{j} - 2\hat{k}$$

$$|\vec{r}| = \sqrt{1 + 4 + 4} = 3$$<p>Magnitude factor:</p>$$\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}\frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2} = \frac{9\times10^9\times(3\times10^{-6})(-4\times10^{-6})}{3^2} = -1.2\times10^{-2}\ \text{N}$$<p>Force on $q_2$ (directed along $\hat{r}$ from $q_1\to q_2$):</p>$$\vec{F} = \left(-1.2\times10^{-2}\right)\frac{\vec{r}}{|\vec{r}|} = \left(-1.2\times10^{-2}\right)\frac{(-\hat{i}-2\hat{j}-2\hat{k})}{3}$$

$$\vec{F} = (4\times10^{-3})\hat{i} + (8\times10^{-3})\hat{j} + (8\times10^{-3})\hat{k}$$<p>The charges are opposite, so $q_2$ is pulled toward $q_1$ along $(+\hat{i}+2\hat{j}+2\hat{k})$, as obtained.</p>$$\vec{F} = (4\hat{i} + 8\hat{j} + 8\hat{k})\times10^{-3}\ \text{N}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B ($(4\hat{i} + 8\hat{j} + 8\hat{k}) \times 10^{-3}$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 8 Apr, Shift 2

A parallel plate capacitor is having separation between plates 0.885 mm. It has a capacitance of 1 $\mu$F when the space between the plates is filled with an insulating material of resistivity $1 \times 10^{13}$ $\Omega$m and resistance $17.7 \times 10^{14}$ $\Omega$. Relative permittivity of the insulating material is $\alpha \times 10^7$. The value of $\alpha$ is __________. (Take permittivity of free space $= 8.85 \times 10^{-12}$ F/m)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Find the plate area</strong> from the resistance of the dielectric slab ($R = \rho\, d / A$):</p>$$A = \frac{\rho\, d}{R} = \frac{(10^{13})(0.885\times10^{-3})}{17.7\times10^{14}} = 5\times10^{-6}\ \text{m}^2$$<p><strong>Use the capacitance formula</strong> $C = \dfrac{\epsilon_0 \epsilon_r A}{d}$:</p>$$\epsilon_r = \frac{C\,d}{\epsilon_0 A} = \frac{(10^{-6})(0.885\times10^{-3})}{(8.85\times10^{-12})(5\times10^{-6})}$$

$$\epsilon_r = \frac{8.85\times10^{-10}}{4.425\times10^{-17}} = 2\times10^{7}$$<p>So $\alpha\times10^7 = 2\times10^7 \Rightarrow \alpha = 2$.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> 2</p></div></details>
