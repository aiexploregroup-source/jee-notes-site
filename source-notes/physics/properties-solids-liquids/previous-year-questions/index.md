The increase in the pressure required to decrease the volume $(\Delta V)$ of water is $6.3 \times 10^7\ \text{N/m}^2$. The percentage decrease in the volume is _____. (Bulk modulus of water $= 2.1 \times 10^9\ \text{N/m}^2$.)

A spherical liquid drop of radius $R$ acquires the terminal velocity $v_1$ when falls through a gas of viscosity $\eta$. Now the drop is broken into 64 identical droplets and each droplet acquires terminal velocity $v_2$ falling through the same gas. The ratio of terminal velocities $v_1/v_2$ is __________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Terminal velocity from Stokes’ law (drag balancing weight) scales as the square of the radius:</p>$$v_t = \frac{2}{9}\frac{r^2(\rho - \sigma)g}{\eta} \implies v_t \propto r^2$$<p>Volume conservation for $n = 64$ identical droplets:</p>$$r = \frac{R}{n^{1/3}} = \frac{R}{64^{1/3}} = \frac{R}{4}$$<p>Therefore:</p>$$\frac{v_1}{v_2} = \left(\frac{R}{r}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{R}{R/4}\right)^2 = 4^2 = 16$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> D (16)</p></div></details>
