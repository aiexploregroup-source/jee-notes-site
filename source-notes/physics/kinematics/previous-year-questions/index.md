PYQ · 2026 4 Apr, Shift 1

The two projectiles are projected with the same initial velocities at the $15^\circ$ and $30^\circ$ with respect to the horizontal. The ratio of their ranges is $1:x$. The value of $x$ is

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The horizontal range of a projectile is</p>$$R = \frac{u^2 \sin 2\theta}{g}$$<p>With the same $u$ and $g$, the range depends only on $\sin 2\theta$:</p>$$\frac{R_1}{R_2} = \frac{\sin(2\times 15^\circ)}{\sin(2\times 30^\circ)} = \frac{\sin 30^\circ}{\sin 60^\circ} = \frac{1/2}{\sqrt{3}/2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$$<p>So the ratio is $1 : \sqrt{3}$, giving $x = \sqrt{3}$.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> B ($\sqrt{3}$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 2 Apr, Shift 1

The velocity of a particle is given as $\vec{v} = -x\hat{i} + 2y\hat{j} - z\hat{k}$ m/s. The magnitude of acceleration at point $(1, 2, 4)$ is __________ m/s$^2$.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The velocity depends on position, so acceleration is found using the chain rule component-wise, $a_i = v_i\,\dfrac{\partial v_i}{\partial x_i}$ (each component of velocity depends only on its own coordinate):</p>$$a_x = v_x\frac{\partial v_x}{\partial x} = (-x)(-1) = x$$

$$a_y = v_y\frac{\partial v_y}{\partial y} = (2y)(2) = 4y$$

$$a_z = v_z\frac{\partial v_z}{\partial z} = (-z)(-1) = z$$<p>At $(1, 2, 4)$:</p>$$\vec{a} = (1)\hat{i} + (8)\hat{j} + (4)\hat{k}$$

$$|\vec{a}| = \sqrt{1^2 + 8^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{1 + 64 + 16} = \sqrt{81} = 9\ \text{m/s}^2$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B (9)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 4 Apr, Shift 2

Two cars $A$ and $B$ are moving in the same direction along a straight line with speeds 100 km/h and 80 km/h, respectively such that car $A$ is moving ahead of car $B$. A person in car $B$ throws a stone with a speed $v$ so that it hits the car $A$ with a speed of 5 m/s. The value of $v$ is __________ km/h.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Work with speeds relative to the ground and take the direction of motion as positive. Car $A$ (ahead) moves at $100$ km/h and car $B$ at $80$ km/h.</p><p>The stone is thrown forward from $B$ with speed $v$ relative to $B$, so its ground speed is $(80 + v)$ km/h.</p><p>It hits $A$ with a relative speed of $5$ m/s. Convert:</p>$$5\ \text{m/s} = 5 \times 3.6 = 18\ \text{km/h}$$<p>The impact speed is the stone’s speed relative to $A$:</p>$$(80 + v) - 100 = 18$$

$$v = 18 + 20 = 38\ \text{km/h}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> C (38)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 4 Apr, Shift 2

If $x$ and $y$ coordinates of a projectile as a function of time ($t$) are given as $24t$ and $43.6t - 4.9t^2$, respectively, then the angle (in degrees) made by the projectile with horizontal when $t = 2$ s is __________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The velocity components are the time derivatives of the coordinates:</p>$$v_x = \frac{dx}{dt} = 24\ \text{m/s}$$

$$v_y = \frac{dy}{dt} = 43.6 - 9.8t$$<p>At $t = 2$ s:</p>$$v_y = 43.6 - 9.8(2) = 43.6 - 19.6 = 24\ \text{m/s}$$<p>The angle with the horizontal:</p>$$\tan\theta = \frac{v_y}{v_x} = \frac{24}{24} = 1 \implies \theta = 45^\circ$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B (45)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 4 Apr, Shift 2

A gun mounted on the ground fires bullets in all directions with same speed. The farthest distance the bullets could reach is 6.4 m. The speed of the bullets from the gun is __________ m/s. (take $g = 10$ m/s$^2$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The farthest horizontal distance corresponds to the maximum range, achieved at a launch angle of $45^\circ$:</p>$$R_{\max} = \frac{u^2}{g}$$<p>Solving for $u$:</p>$$u = \sqrt{R_{\max}\,g} = \sqrt{6.4 \times 10} = \sqrt{64} = 8\ \text{m/s}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 8</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 5 Apr, Shift 1

From 18 m height above the ground a ball is dropped from rest. The height above the ground at which the magnitude of velocity equal to the magnitude of acceleration (in the same set of units) due to gravity is ______ m.
(Take $g = 10$ m/s$^2$ and neglect the air resistance)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The ball is dropped from rest, so we need the point where its speed equals the numerical value of $g$, i.e. $v = 10$ m/s.</p><p>Using $v^2 = u^2 + 2g\,h_{\text{fall}}$ with $u = 0$:</p>$$10^2 = 2(10)\,h_{\text{fall}} \implies h_{\text{fall}} = \frac{100}{20} = 5\ \text{m}$$<p>Height above the ground:</p>$$h = 18 - 5 = 13\ \text{m}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 13</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 8 Apr, Shift 2

A gas balloon is going up with a constant velocity of 10 m/s. When this balloon reached a height of 75 m, a stone is dropped from it and balloon keeps moving up with the same velocity. The height of the balloon when the stone hits the ground is __________ m. (Take $g = 10$ m/s$^2$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>When released, the stone shares the balloon’s upward velocity, so its initial velocity is $+10$ m/s (upward). Take upward as positive with the origin at the ground.</p><p>Stone’s position: $y = 75 + 10t - 5t^2$. It hits the ground when $y = 0$:</p>$$5t^2 - 10t - 75 = 0 \implies t^2 - 2t - 15 = 0$$

$$t = \frac{2 + \sqrt{4 + 60}}{2} = \frac{2 + 8}{2} = 5\ \text{s}$$<p>Meanwhile the balloon rises at $10$ m/s from $75$ m:</p>$$h_{\text{balloon}} = 75 + 10 \times 5 = 125\ \text{m}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> D (125)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 8 Apr, Shift 2

Two identical bodies, projected with the same speed at two different angles cover the same horizontal range $R$. If the time of flight of these bodies are 5 s and 10 s, respectively, then the value of $R$ is __________ m. (Take $g = 10$ m/s$^2$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Equal ranges at the same speed occur for complementary launch angles $\theta$ and $90^\circ - \theta$. Their times of flight are</p>$$T_1 = \frac{2u\sin\theta}{g}, \qquad T_2 = \frac{2u\cos\theta}{g}$$<p>Multiplying:</p>$$T_1 T_2 = \frac{4u^2\sin\theta\cos\theta}{g^2} = \frac{2u^2\sin 2\theta}{g^2} = \frac{2R}{g}$$<p>since $R = \dfrac{u^2\sin 2\theta}{g}$. Solving for $R$:</p>$$R = \frac{g\,T_1 T_2}{2} = \frac{10 \times 5 \times 10}{2} = 250\ \text{m}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> A (250)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 8 Apr, Shift 2

Two masses of 3.4 kg and 2.5 kg are accelerated from an initial speed of 5 m/s and 12 m/s, respectively. The distances traversed by the masses in the 5th second are 104 m and 129 m, respectively. The ratio of their momenta after 10 s is $\frac{x}{8}$. The value of $x$ is __________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The distance covered in the $n$th second is $S_n = u + \dfrac{a}{2}(2n - 1)$. For $n = 5$, this factor is $\dfrac{a}{2}(9) = 4.5a$.</p><p>Mass 1 ($u_1 = 5$):</p>$$5 + 4.5a_1 = 104 \implies a_1 = \frac{99}{4.5} = 22\ \text{m/s}^2$$<p>Mass 2 ($u_2 = 12$):</p>$$12 + 4.5a_2 = 129 \implies a_2 = \frac{117}{4.5} = 26\ \text{m/s}^2$$<p>Velocities after $10$ s using $v = u + at$:</p>$$v_1 = 5 + 22(10) = 225\ \text{m/s}, \qquad v_2 = 12 + 26(10) = 272\ \text{m/s}$$<p>Momenta:</p>$$p_1 = 3.4 \times 225 = 765\ \text{kg}\cdot\text{m/s}, \qquad p_2 = 2.5 \times 272 = 680\ \text{kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$$

$$\frac{p_1}{p_2} = \frac{765}{680} = \frac{9}{8} = \frac{x}{8} \implies x = 9$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 9</p></div></details>
