PYQ · 2026 Apr 4, Shift 1

An ideal gas undergoes a process maintaining relation between pressure $(P)$ and volume $(V)$ as $P = P_0\left(1 + \left(\frac{V_0}{V}\right)^2\right)^{-1}$, where $P_0$ and $V_0$ are constants. If two samples $A$ and $B$ (two moles each) with initial volumes $V_0$ and $3V_0$ respectively undergo above mentioned process, then the difference of temperatures of these samples, $T_B - T_A$ is _____. ($R$ = gas constant)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>For $n$ moles, $T = \dfrac{PV}{nR}$ with $n = 2$, so $nR\,T = PV$.</p><p><strong>Sample A</strong> at $V = V_0$:</p>$$P_A = \frac{P_0}{1 + (V_0/V_0)^2} = \frac{P_0}{2}, \qquad P_A V_0 = \frac{P_0 V_0}{2}$$<p><strong>Sample B</strong> at $V = 3V_0$:</p>$$P_B = \frac{P_0}{1 + (V_0/3V_0)^2} = \frac{P_0}{1 + \tfrac{1}{9}} = \frac{9P_0}{10}, \qquad P_B (3V_0) = \frac{27 P_0 V_0}{10}$$<p><strong>Temperature difference</strong> ($n = 2$):</p>$$T_B - T_A = \frac{P_B V_B - P_A V_A}{2R} = \frac{1}{2R}\left(\frac{27P_0 V_0}{10} - \frac{P_0 V_0}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{2R}\cdot\frac{22P_0 V_0}{10} = \frac{11 P_0 V_0}{10R}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> B ($\frac{11P_0 V_0}{10R}$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 2, Shift 1

Heat is supplied to a diatomic gas at constant pressure. Then the ratio of $\Delta Q : \Delta U : \Delta W$ is __________.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>For a diatomic gas, $C_V = \dfrac{5}{2}R$ and $C_P = \dfrac{7}{2}R$. At constant pressure, for $n$ moles over $\Delta T$:</p>$$\Delta Q = nC_P\,\Delta T = \frac{7}{2}nR\,\Delta T$$<p></p>$$\Delta U = nC_V\,\Delta T = \frac{5}{2}nR\,\Delta T$$<p></p>$$\Delta W = P\,\Delta V = nR\,\Delta T$$<p>Dividing each by $nR\,\Delta T$:</p>$$\Delta Q : \Delta U : \Delta W = \frac{7}{2} : \frac{5}{2} : 1 = 7 : 5 : 2$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> D ($7 : 5 : 2$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 2, Shift 1

A vessel contains 0.15 m$^3$ of a gas at pressure 8 bar and temperature 140 °C with $c_p = 3R$ and $c_v = 2R$. It is expanded adiabatically till pressure falls to 1 bar. The work done during this process is __________ kJ. ($R$ is gas constant)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>$\gamma = \dfrac{c_p}{c_v} = \dfrac{3R}{2R} = \dfrac{3}{2}$.</p><p>Given $P_1 = 8 \times 10^5\ \text{Pa}$, $V_1 = 0.15\ \text{m}^3$, $P_2 = 1 \times 10^5\ \text{Pa}$.</p><p><strong>Final volume</strong> (adiabatic, $P_1 V_1^{\gamma} = P_2 V_2^{\gamma}$):</p>$$V_2 = V_1\left(\frac{P_1}{P_2}\right)^{1/\gamma} = 0.15 \times 8^{2/3} = 0.15 \times 4 = 0.60\ \text{m}^3$$<p><strong>Work done in adiabatic process:</strong></p>$$W = \frac{P_1 V_1 - P_2 V_2}{\gamma - 1} = \frac{(8\times10^5)(0.15) - (1\times10^5)(0.60)}{1.5 - 1}$$<p></p>$$W = \frac{1.2\times10^5 - 0.6\times10^5}{0.5} = \frac{0.6\times10^5}{0.5} = 1.2\times10^5\ \text{J} = 120\ \text{kJ}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 120</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 2, Shift 2

5 moles of unknown gas is heated at constant volume from $10\,°C$ to $20\,°C$. The molar specific heat of this gas at constant pressure $c_p = 8$ cal/mol·$°C$ and $R = 8.36$ J/mol·$°C$. The change in the internal energy of the gas is __________ calorie.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Convert $R$ to calories ($1\ \text{cal} = 4.18\ \text{J}$):</p>$$R = \frac{8.36}{4.18} = 2\ \text{cal/mol}\cdot°C$$<p><strong>Molar specific heat at constant volume</strong> (Mayer’s relation):</p>$$c_v = c_p - R = 8 - 2 = 6\ \text{cal/mol}\cdot°C$$<p><strong>Change in internal energy</strong> ($n = 5$, $\Delta T = 20 - 10 = 10\,°C$):</p>$$\Delta U = n\,c_v\,\Delta T = 5 \times 6 \times 10 = 300\ \text{cal}$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 300</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 6, Shift 2

A cylinder with adiabatic walls is closed at both ends and is divided into two compartments by a frictionless adiabatic piston. Ideal gas is filled in both (left and right) the compartments at same $P,V,T$. Heating is started from left side until pressure changes to $\dfrac{27P}{8}$. If initial volume of each compartment was 9 litres then the final volume in right-hand side compartment is __________ litres. (for this ideal gas $C_P/C_V=1.5$)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The piston is frictionless, so pressure equalises across it; the common final pressure is $P_f = \dfrac{27P}{8}$.</p><p>The <strong>right compartment</strong> is compressed <strong>adiabatically</strong> (its walls and the piston are adiabatic), so $PV^{\gamma}$ is conserved with $\gamma = 1.5$:</p>$$P\,V_i^{\gamma} = P_f\,V_f^{\gamma} \implies V_f = V_i\left(\frac{P}{P_f}\right)^{1/\gamma}$$<p></p>$$V_f = 9\left(\frac{P}{27P/8}\right)^{1/1.5} = 9\left(\frac{8}{27}\right)^{2/3} = 9 \times \frac{4}{9} = 4\ \text{litres}$$<p>since $\left(\dfrac{8}{27}\right)^{2/3} = \dfrac{8^{2/3}}{27^{2/3}} = \dfrac{4}{9}$.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> B ($4$)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 5, Shift 1

Given below are two statements: one is labelled as Assertion A and the other is labelled as Reason R.

**Statement I:** Change in internal energy of a system containing $n$ mole of ideal gas can be written as $\Delta U = n\,C_V(T_f - T_i) = \dfrac{nR}{\gamma - 1}(T_f - T_i)$, where $\gamma = \dfrac{C_p}{C_V}$, $T_i$ = initial temperature, $T_f$ = final temperature.

**Statement II:** Relation between degree of freedom $f$ and $\gamma$ ($= C_p/C_V$) is $\left( \gamma = 1 + \dfrac{2}{f} \right)$.

Choose the correct answer from the options given below.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>Statement I:</strong> For an ideal gas, $C_P - C_V = R$ and $\gamma = C_P/C_V$, so</p>$$C_V = \frac{R}{\gamma - 1} \implies \Delta U = nC_V\,\Delta T = \frac{nR}{\gamma-1}\,\Delta T$$<p>This is <strong>true</strong>.</p><p><strong>Statement II:</strong> With $f$ degrees of freedom, $C_V = \dfrac{f}{2}R$ and $C_P = \dfrac{f+2}{2}R$, giving</p>$$\gamma = \frac{C_P}{C_V} = \frac{f+2}{f} = 1 + \frac{2}{f}$$<p>This is also <strong>true</strong>.</p><p>However, the second equality in Statement I follows from $C_V = R/(\gamma-1)$ (Mayer’s relation), which does <strong>not</strong> require the degrees-of-freedom relation of Statement II. Hence R is true but is <strong>not</strong> the correct explanation of A.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> B (Both A and R are true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 5, Shift 1

Consider the following statements:

A. Zeroth law of thermodynamics gives concept of temperature
B. First law of thermodynamics gives concept of internal energy
C. In isothermal expansion of ideal gas, $\Delta Q \neq \Delta W$
D. Product of intensive and extensive variables is extensive
E. The ratio of any extensive variable to mass will be an extensive variable

Choose the correct combination of statements from the options given below.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p><strong>A — True.</strong> The zeroth law establishes thermal equilibrium and hence the concept of temperature.</p><p><strong>B — True.</strong> The first law, $\Delta Q = \Delta U + \Delta W$, introduces internal energy $U$ as a state function.</p><p><strong>C — False.</strong> For isothermal expansion of an ideal gas, $\Delta U = 0$, so $\Delta Q = \Delta W$ (not $\neq$).</p><p><strong>D — True.</strong> Intensive $\times$ extensive is extensive (e.g. density $\times$ volume $=$ mass).</p><p><strong>E — False.</strong> Extensive $\div$ mass gives a specific (intensive) quantity, not extensive.</p><p>Correct statements: <strong>A, B and D</strong>.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> C (A, B and D Only)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 5, Shift 2

The heat extracted out of $x$ gram of water initially at $50\,^\circ$C to cool it down to $0\,^\circ$C is sufficient to evaporate $(1000 - x)$ gram of water also initially at $50\,^\circ$C. The value of $x$ (closest integer) is __________. (Take latent heat of water 2256 kJ/kg, specific heat capacity of water 4200 J/kg·K)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>Let $c = 4200\ \text{J/kg}\cdot\text{K}$ and $L = 2256\times10^3\ \text{J/kg}$.</p><p><strong>Heat released</strong> cooling $x$ g from $50\,°C$ to $0\,°C$ ($\Delta T = 50\ \text{K}$):</p>$$Q_1 = \frac{x}{1000}\,c\,(50)$$<p><strong>Heat needed</strong> to evaporate $(1000 - x)$ g of water:</p>$$Q_2 = \frac{1000 - x}{1000}\,L$$<p>Setting $Q_1 = Q_2$ (the mass factor $1/1000$ cancels):</p>$$x\,c\,(50) = (1000 - x)\,L$$<p></p>$$x(4200 \times 50) = (1000 - x)(2256\times10^3)$$<p></p>$$210000\,x = 2.256\times10^9 - 2256000\,x$$<p></p>$$x(210000 + 2256000) = 2.256\times10^9 \implies x = \frac{2.256\times10^9}{2466000} \approx 914.8$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> 915</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 8, Shift 2

One mole of diatomic gas having rotational modes only is kept in a cylinder with a piston system. The cross-section area of the cylinder is 4 cm$^2$. The gas is heated slowly to raise the temperature by 1.2 $^\circ$C during which the piston moves by 25 mm. The amount of heat supplied to the gas is __________ J. (Atmospheric pressure = 100 kPa, $R = 8.3$ J/mol·K) (Neglect mass of the piston)

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>The piston is massless and moves quasi-statically against atmospheric pressure, so the gas pressure stays equal to $P_{atm}$ and the <strong>work done by the gas</strong> is:</p>$$\Delta W = P_{atm}\,\Delta V = P_{atm}\,(A\cdot d)$$<p></p>$$\Delta W = (100\times10^3)\,(4\times10^{-4})\,(25\times10^{-3}) = 1.0\ \text{J}$$<p>A diatomic molecule with translational and rotational modes (no vibration) has $f = 5$, so $C_V = \dfrac{5}{2}R$. The <strong>change in internal energy</strong> for $n = 1$ mole is:</p>$$\Delta U = nC_V\,\Delta T = 1 \times \frac{5}{2}(8.3)(1.2) = 24.9\ \text{J}$$<p><strong>Heat supplied</strong> (first law, $\Delta Q = \Delta U + \Delta W$):</p>$$\Delta Q = 24.9 + 1.0 = 25.9\ \text{J} \approx 25\ \text{J}$$<p>Rounding to the given options, the heat supplied is $\approx 25$ J.</p><p><strong>Answer:</strong> B (25)</p></div></details>

PYQ · 2026 Apr 8, Shift 2

Initial pressure and volume of a monoatomic ideal gas are $P$ and $V$. The change in internal energy of this gas in adiabatic expansion to volume $V_{final} = 27V$ is __________ J.

<details class="solution"><p>**Solution / Answer**</p><summary class="solution-summary"><span class="solution-btn">Show solution</span></summary><div class="solution-body"><p>For a monoatomic gas, $\gamma = \dfrac{5}{3}$ and $C_V = \dfrac{3}{2}R$.</p><p><strong>Final pressure</strong> (adiabatic, $PV^{\gamma} = $ const):</p>$$P_f = P\left(\frac{V}{27V}\right)^{5/3} = P\,(27)^{-5/3} = P\,(3^3)^{-5/3} = P\cdot 3^{-5} = \frac{P}{243}$$<p><strong>Product $P_f V_f$:</strong></p>$$P_f V_f = \frac{P}{243}\times 27V = \frac{27}{243}PV = \frac{PV}{9}$$<p><strong>Change in internal energy</strong> (using $\Delta U = nC_V\Delta T = \frac{C_V}{R}(P_f V_f - PV) = \frac{3}{2}(P_f V_f - PV)$):</p>$$\Delta U = \frac{3}{2}\left(\frac{PV}{9} - PV\right) = \frac{3}{2}\left(-\frac{8}{9}PV\right) = -\frac{4}{3}PV$$<p><strong>Answer:</strong> C ($-\frac{4}{3}PV$)</p></div></details>
